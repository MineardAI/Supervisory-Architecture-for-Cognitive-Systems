# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and constructive community. Participants must not engage in harassment, discrimination, intimidation, personal attacks, deliberate disruption, unwanted sexual attention, doxxing, or publication of private information.

Technical disagreement is welcome. Critique ideas, code, and evidence directly and respectfully. Do not claim authority, verification, or project endorsement that you do not possess.

## Scope

This code applies in project spaces, issues, pull requests, discussions, and public communications when representing the project.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainers through the repository’s private reporting or security-reporting channel. Do not include sensitive personal information in a public issue. Reports will be reviewed fairly and confidentially where practicable.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may clarify, moderate, remove content, restrict participation, or take other proportionate action. Retaliation against a good-faith reporter is not acceptable.
