SACS Contract 011 — Realization Certification, Supervisory Provenance, and Audit
Section 5 — Historical and Provenance Principles
Purpose

Section 5 establishes the constitutional principles governing the construction, certification, preservation, and disclosure of supervisory history within SACS.

These principles apply uniformly across Realization Certification, Supervisory Provenance, Replay, Audit, Retention, and every historical artifact produced by Contract 011.

Every subsequent section SHALL preserve these principles.

Principle of Constitutional History

Contract 011 establishes constitutional history.

Constitutional history is the authoritative historical representation that can be established solely from committed constitutional artifacts produced by preceding contracts.

Accordingly:

constitutional history SHALL be derived exclusively from committed evidence;
constitutional history SHALL preserve constitutional authority;
constitutional history SHALL remain immutable after commitment.

Constitutional history SHALL NOT be interpreted as a claim regarding objective external reality.

Principle of Evidence Sufficiency

Historical certification SHALL be based only upon admissible committed constitutional evidence.

The absence of evidence SHALL NOT constitute evidence of absence.

Likewise, incomplete evidence SHALL NOT authorize inference, reconstruction, or silent completion.

Where sufficient committed evidence does not exist, Contract 011 SHALL produce the appropriate committed failure defined by this contract.

Principle of Historical Immutability

Once committed, every historical artifact produced by Contract 011 SHALL become immutable.

Historical artifacts SHALL NOT be:

edited;
rewritten;
replaced;
reclassified;
silently corrected;
reconstructed.

Corrections to supervisory history SHALL occur only through the commitment of new constitutional artifacts defined by later constitutional authority.

Previously committed historical artifacts SHALL remain permanently preserved.

Principle of Provenance Preservation

Every historical conclusion SHALL preserve its constitutional lineage.

Each historical artifact SHALL remain traceable to:

the committed artifacts from which it was derived;
the constitutional authorities responsible for each transformation;
the registries, schemas, and definitions governing its construction.

Loss of provenance SHALL constitute loss of constitutional integrity.

Principle of Authority Preservation

Contract 011 SHALL preserve the authority established by every preceding contract.

Historical construction SHALL organize constitutional authority.

Historical construction SHALL NOT create constitutional authority.

Accordingly:

authority SHALL be preserved;
authority SHALL remain attributable;
authority SHALL remain uniquely owned.

Authority SHALL NOT be expanded, diminished, transferred, or reinterpreted.

Principle of Non-Reconstruction

Missing constitutional history SHALL remain explicitly missing.

Contract 011 SHALL NOT:

infer missing events;
reconstruct missing artifacts;
fabricate historical continuity;
replace unavailable evidence;
substitute newer definitions for historical ones.

Historical incompleteness SHALL be represented explicitly.

It SHALL NOT be hidden.

Principle of Deterministic History

Identical committed constitutional evidence SHALL always produce identical constitutional history.

For identical:

committed artifacts;
registry snapshots;
schemas;
lineage rules;
certification context;
provenance rules;

every conforming implementation SHALL produce identical:

Realization Records;
Supervisory Cycle Records;
Replay results;
Audit disclosures.

Implementation architecture SHALL NOT alter constitutional history.

Principle of Temporal Preservation

Contract 011 SHALL preserve constitutional temporal ordering exactly as represented by committed supervisory evidence.

Historical construction SHALL NOT:

reorder supervisory events;
compress distinct constitutional events;
merge independent supervisory cycles;
invent temporal relationships.

Where temporal uncertainty exists, that uncertainty SHALL be preserved explicitly.

Principle of Disclosure Integrity

Historical disclosure SHALL expose committed constitutional history without modification.

Every disclosure SHALL preserve:

artifact identity;
authority;
lineage;
integrity references;
commitment identity.

Disclosure SHALL NOT:

reinterpret constitutional meaning;
derive supervisory conclusions;
certify external truth;
omit required lineage relationships.

Disclosure is informational.

It is not adjudicative.

Principle of Replay Fidelity

Replay reconstructs constitutional computation.

Replay SHALL NOT recreate runtime execution.

Accordingly, replay SHALL:

consume committed historical artifacts;
preserve historical registries;
preserve historical schemas;
preserve historical definitions;
preserve historical numeric interpretation.

Replay SHALL NOT:

substitute current registries;
execute runtime actions;
repair historical inconsistencies;
infer unavailable historical evidence.

Replay demonstrates constitutional reproducibility.

It does not recreate physical history.

Principle of Historical Finality

Every supervisory realization SHALL terminate in one—and only one—authoritative constitutional historical outcome.

Contract 011 SHALL therefore produce either:

one Committed Realization Record or one committed realization-certification failure;

followed, where applicable, by:

one Committed Supervisory Cycle Record or one committed provenance or cycle-commitment failure.

No supervisory realization SHALL remain constitutionally unresolved.

Historical Doctrine

The governing doctrine of Contract 011 is:

Historical authority within SACS arises exclusively from committed constitutional evidence. Contract 011 preserves, certifies, and organizes that evidence into immutable constitutional history without reconstruction, reinterpretation, or expansion of supervisory authority.
