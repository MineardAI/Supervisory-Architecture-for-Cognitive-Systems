SACS Contract 011 — Realization Certification, Supervisory Provenance, and Audit
Section 4 — Canonical Inputs, Outputs, and Two-Stage Commitment Model
Purpose

Section 4 establishes the canonical information flow, commitment boundaries, and constitutional transformation model of Contract 011.

It defines the authoritative artifacts consumed and produced by this contract and formally establishes the two-stage commitment doctrine governing the transformation of supervisory execution history into authoritative constitutional history.

Every subsequent section of this contract SHALL preserve this model.

Constitutional Information Flow

Contract 011 receives only committed constitutional artifacts from preceding contracts.

It performs two sequential constitutional transformations.

The first establishes authoritative realization history.

The second establishes authoritative whole-cycle constitutional lineage.

Verification

No implementation SHALL alter this constitutional ordering.

Canonical Inputs

The required canonical inputs for Stage One are:

Committed Execution Disposition
Committed Supervisory Continuity Record

These artifacts SHALL be accepted exactly as committed by their originating contracts.

Contract 011 SHALL NOT modify, reinterpret, or replace them.

During Stage Two, Contract 011 MAY additionally resolve previously committed constitutional artifacts required to construct complete supervisory lineage, including but not limited to:

Committed Operational Profile
Committed Reference State
Committed Control Error
Committed Control Command
Committed Translation Transaction
Committed Execution Disposition
Committed Supervisory Continuity Record
Committed Realization Record

These artifacts SHALL remain immutable throughout provenance construction.

Stage One Commitment Boundary

Stage One terminates with one—and only one—constitutional commitment.

Successful certification SHALL produce exactly one:

Committed Realization Record

If certification cannot be completed, Stage One SHALL instead produce exactly one committed realization-certification failure.

Stage Two SHALL NOT begin unless a Committed Realization Record exists.

Realization-certification failures SHALL terminate Stage One.

They SHALL NOT enter Stage Two.

Stage Two Commitment Boundary

Stage Two consumes only:

the Committed Realization Record; and
previously committed constitutional artifacts required for lineage construction.

Successful provenance assembly SHALL produce exactly one:

Committed Supervisory Cycle Record

If provenance cannot be completed, Stage Two SHALL instead produce exactly one committed provenance or cycle-commitment failure.

No additional constitutional commitment exists within Contract 011.

Commitment Independence

The two commitment boundaries serve different constitutional purposes.

The first commitment establishes authoritative realization history.

The second commitment establishes authoritative constitutional lineage.

Neither commitment SHALL replace or invalidate the other.

The Committed Supervisory Cycle Record SHALL reference the Committed Realization Record as one of its constituent historical authorities.

It SHALL NOT recreate or supersede it.

Commitment Preconditions

Stage One SHALL begin only when all required committed inputs are available.

Stage Two SHALL begin only after successful completion of Stage One.

Accordingly:

Stage Two SHALL NOT consume uncommitted realization artifacts.
Stage Two SHALL NOT certify realization history.
Stage One SHALL NOT construct supervisory lineage.

Each stage SHALL remain constitutionally independent while preserving deterministic sequencing.

Canonical Artifact Ownership

Ownership of every canonical artifact SHALL remain unique.

Artifact	Constitutional Owner
Committed Execution Disposition	Contract 009
Committed Supervisory Continuity Record	Contract 010
Committed Realization Record	Contract 011 (Stage One)
Committed Supervisory Cycle Record	Contract 011 (Stage Two)

Contract 011 SHALL preserve the ownership of all referenced artifacts.

Construction of the Supervisory Cycle Record SHALL NOT transfer ownership of constituent artifacts.

Atomic Commitment

Each constitutional commitment performed by Contract 011 SHALL be atomic.

A commitment SHALL either:

complete successfully and become immutable; or
fail explicitly without producing a partially committed canonical artifact.

Partial commitment SHALL NOT occur.

Rollback SHALL NOT create alternate historical representations.

Retry SHALL produce the same committed artifact or the same explicit committed failure when supplied with identical committed inputs.

Information Preservation

Throughout both constitutional stages, Contract 011 SHALL preserve:

artifact identity;
authority;
provenance;
temporal ordering;
uncertainty representation;
integrity references;
commitment identity;
originating contract ownership.

Information MAY be organized into canonical historical structures.

Information SHALL NOT be silently added, removed, inferred, repaired, or reinterpreted.

Two-Stage Commitment Principle

The defining commitment principle of Contract 011 is:

Contract 011 SHALL transform committed supervisory execution history into constitutional history through two sequential and independent commitment boundaries. The first SHALL establish exactly one authoritative Realization Record or one explicit realization-certification failure. Only a committed Realization Record may enter the second stage, which SHALL establish exactly one authoritative Supervisory Cycle Record or one explicit provenance or cycle-commitment failure.
