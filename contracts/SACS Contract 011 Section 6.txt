SACS Contract 011 — Realization Certification, Supervisory Provenance, and Audit
Part II — Realization Certification
Section 6 — Execution and Continuity Evidence Intake
Purpose

Section 6 establishes the constitutional intake authority for Realization Certification.

Its purpose is to receive, identify, and bind the committed constitutional evidence required to begin realization certification.

This section determines whether the required committed historical evidence exists and belongs to the same supervisory realization context.

It does not determine admissibility, certification, historical outcome, or provenance.

Constitutional Responsibility

Execution and Continuity Evidence Intake SHALL:

receive the committed constitutional inputs required for realization certification;
verify that all mandatory constitutional inputs are present;
establish the supervisory realization context;
bind received artifacts to a single realization-certification context;
preserve the identity and integrity of every received artifact.

Successful completion of this section authorizes progression to Evidence Identity and Binding Validation.

Required Constitutional Inputs

Execution and Continuity Evidence Intake SHALL require exactly one of each of the following committed artifacts:

Committed Execution Disposition (Contract 009)
Committed Supervisory Continuity Record (Contract 010)

Each artifact SHALL already possess:

immutable commitment identity;
constitutional authority;
originating contract identity;
commitment timestamp;
integrity reference.

No additional supervisory authority may be introduced during intake.

Intake Context

Every realization-certification process SHALL establish exactly one Realization Certification Context.

The context SHALL uniquely identify:

supervisory cycle identity;
realization identity;
execution identity;
supervisory continuity identity;
applicable constitutional version;
registry snapshot references;
schema version references.

All subsequent sections SHALL operate exclusively within this established context.

No section may silently change the realization-certification context.

Intake Validation

Execution and Continuity Evidence Intake SHALL determine only:

whether every mandatory committed artifact is present;
whether each artifact is readable;
whether commitment identity is available;
whether required integrity references are present;
whether the artifacts may enter constitutional validation.

This section SHALL NOT determine:

admissibility;
authenticity;
certification;
supervisory correctness;
lineage completeness;
replay eligibility.

Those determinations belong to later sections.

Intake Preservation

Upon successful intake:

every received artifact SHALL remain immutable;
artifact identifiers SHALL remain unchanged;
commitment identifiers SHALL remain unchanged;
originating authority SHALL remain unchanged;
integrity references SHALL remain unchanged.

Contract 011 SHALL preserve every received artifact exactly as committed.

Intake Failure

Execution and Continuity Evidence Intake SHALL terminate immediately if any mandatory constitutional input is unavailable.

Representative failure conditions include:

REALIZATION_INPUT_MISSING

EXECUTION_DISPOSITION_MISSING

SUPERVISORY_CONTINUITY_RECORD_MISSING

COMMITMENT_IDENTIFIER_MISSING

INTEGRITY_REFERENCE_MISSING

CONSTITUTIONAL_VERSION_UNAVAILABLE

This section SHALL NOT attempt to:

reconstruct missing inputs;
substitute alternate artifacts;
request newer committed artifacts;
infer absent supervisory evidence.

Failure SHALL be represented explicitly.

Constitutional Output

Successful completion of this section SHALL produce exactly one:

Realization Evidence Intake Record

The Realization Evidence Intake Record SHALL contain:

realization-certification context;
identities of received committed artifacts;
commitment identifiers;
originating contract identities;
registry references;
schema references;
intake timestamp;
intake completion status.

The Intake Record is not a committed historical artifact.

It is an internal constitutional working artifact that authorizes progression to the next stage.

Architectural Constraints

Execution and Continuity Evidence Intake SHALL remain a pure intake authority.

It SHALL NOT:

certify realization;
validate evidence authenticity;
evaluate execution outcome;
determine supervisory continuity;
construct historical records;
construct provenance;
perform replay;
perform audit.

Its sole responsibility is establishing a complete and well-defined constitutional intake context.

Fundamental Intake Principle

The defining principle of this section is:

Execution and Continuity Evidence Intake SHALL establish one complete and immutable realization-certification context using only committed constitutional artifacts. It SHALL determine only the presence and identity of required evidence, introducing no supervisory interpretation, certification, or historical judgment.
