SACS Contract 011 — Realization Certification, Supervisory Provenance, and Audit
Section 2 — Architectural Identity
Purpose

Section 2 establishes the constitutional identity of Contract 011.

It defines the unique architectural responsibility performed by Realization Certification, Supervisory Provenance, and Audit within the Supervisory Architecture for Cognitive Systems (SACS).

This identity governs every subsequent section of this contract and defines the authority that Contract 011 alone may exercise.

Constitutional Identity

Contract 011 is the constitutional authority responsible for transforming committed supervisory execution evidence into authoritative constitutional history.

It performs this transformation through two sequential constitutional stages:

Each stage establishes a distinct constitutional commitment boundary.

No subsequent stage may consume artifacts that have not first been committed by the preceding stage.

Stage One — Realization Certification

The first constitutional responsibility of Contract 011 is to determine whether the committed execution disposition and committed supervisory continuity evidence together establish one authoritative supervisory realization.

Realization Certification SHALL:

evaluate only committed constitutional evidence;
preserve the authority established by preceding contracts;
determine whether the evidence is sufficient for constitutional certification;
construct one canonical Realization Record upon successful certification;
commit that record as the exclusive historical realization for the supervisory cycle.

Realization Certification SHALL NOT:

reinterpret supervisory intent;
modify committed execution history;
reconstruct missing evidence;
infer events not represented by committed artifacts;
perform external verification.
Stage Two — Supervisory Provenance

Following successful Realization Certification, Contract 011 SHALL establish the complete constitutional lineage of the supervisory cycle.

Supervisory Provenance SHALL:

resolve committed constitutional artifacts;
validate constitutional relationships;
construct the canonical supervisory lineage;
preserve temporal ordering;
preserve authority relationships;
preserve artifact identity;
commit one authoritative Supervisory Cycle Record.

Supervisory Provenance SHALL NOT:

alter committed artifacts;
create missing history;
replace unresolved evidence;
reinterpret prior supervisory decisions;
reopen constitutional determinations established by earlier contracts.
Historical Authority

Contract 011 establishes constitutional history, not objective reality.

Its authority is limited to determining the authoritative historical representation supported by committed constitutional evidence.

Accordingly:

constitutional certification is not external verification;
historical completeness is not external completeness;
constitutional provenance is not physical causality.

Every historical conclusion produced by this contract SHALL remain bounded by the committed supervisory evidence received from preceding contracts.

Authority Preservation

Contract 011 SHALL preserve every constitutional authority established by previous contracts.

It SHALL NOT:

broaden supervisory authority;
weaken supervisory constraints;
replace committed artifacts;
reinterpret committed semantics;
introduce new supervisory intent.

The authority represented by every committed artifact SHALL remain unchanged throughout realization certification and provenance construction.

Commitment Doctrine

Contract 011 establishes two immutable constitutional commitments.

The first commitment establishes one authoritative historical realization.

Committed Realization Record

The second commitment establishes one authoritative constitutional representation of the complete supervisory cycle.

Committed Supervisory Cycle Record

Neither commitment may invalidate, replace, reinterpret, or reopen the other.

Each committed artifact becomes immutable immediately upon commitment.

Architectural Relationships

Contract 011 occupies the constitutional boundary between supervisory execution and historical verification.

It consumes only committed artifacts from Contracts 009 and 010.

It produces the authoritative historical artifacts upon which Contract 012 performs independent verification.

Accordingly:

Contract 009 determines execution disposition.
Contract 010 determines supervisory continuity.
Contract 011 establishes constitutional history.
Contract 012 evaluates that constitutional history against external evidence without modifying it.

Each contract therefore answers a distinct constitutional question.

Fundamental Architectural Principle

The defining architectural principle of Contract 011 is:

Contract 011 deterministically transforms committed execution and supervisory-continuity evidence into authoritative constitutional history and then transforms that committed realization history into one immutable constitutional representation of the complete supervisory cycle without reopening prior adjudications, reconstructing missing evidence, modifying committed authority, or issuing claims of externally verified truth.
