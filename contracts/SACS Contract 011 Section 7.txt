SACS Contract 011 — Realization Certification, Supervisory Provenance, and Audit
Section 7 — Evidence Identity and Binding Validation
Purpose

Section 7 establishes the constitutional authority responsible for validating that the evidence received during intake belongs to one—and only one—supervisory realization context.

This section confirms the constitutional identity and binding relationships among committed artifacts before historical certification may proceed.

It answers one constitutional question:

Do these committed artifacts represent the same supervisory realization?

This section does not determine evidentiary sufficiency, realization outcome, certification, or historical provenance.

Constitutional Responsibility

Evidence Identity and Binding Validation SHALL:

validate the constitutional identity of every received artifact;
verify that all required artifacts belong to the same supervisory realization context;
validate constitutional binding relationships;
reject cross-cycle or conflicting historical evidence;
preserve the authority of every validated artifact.

Successful completion authorizes progression to Evidence Admissibility Evaluation.

Identity Validation

For every committed artifact received during intake, this section SHALL validate:

commitment identifier;
supervisory cycle identifier;
realization identifier;
originating contract identity;
constitutional version;
schema version;
registry snapshot identity.

Identity validation SHALL confirm that every artifact is uniquely identifiable.

Duplicate constitutional identities SHALL be treated as explicit failure.

Binding Validation

Following successful identity validation, this section SHALL establish constitutional binding.

Binding SHALL verify that:

the Committed Execution Disposition and Committed Supervisory Continuity Record reference the same supervisory cycle;
realization identifiers are consistent;
constitutional authority is preserved across artifacts;
registry references are mutually compatible;
schema versions are constitutionally compatible.

Binding SHALL establish one canonical realization-certification context.

Cross-Contract Consistency

Evidence Identity and Binding Validation SHALL verify consistency across constitutional boundaries.

Representative relationships include:

Contract 009
Committed Execution Disposition
        │
        ├──── Supervisory Cycle Identity
        ├──── Realization Identity
        └──── Commitment Identity

Contract 010
Committed Supervisory Continuity Record
        │
        ├──── Supervisory Cycle Identity
        ├──── Continuity Identity
        └──── Commitment Identity

The supervisory cycle represented by these artifacts SHALL resolve to one unique constitutional realization context.

Binding Constraints

This section SHALL NOT:

determine whether evidence is sufficient;
classify realization outcome;
evaluate execution correctness;
certify supervisory history;
resolve provenance;
infer missing identity information.

Where identity cannot be established explicitly from committed artifacts, validation SHALL fail.

Identity Preservation

Successful validation SHALL preserve:

artifact identifiers;
commitment identifiers;
originating contract identities;
supervisory cycle identities;
realization identities;
registry identities;
schema identities.

No validated identity SHALL be altered by subsequent sections.

Validation Failure

Representative constitutional failures include:

EXECUTION_DISPOSITION_IDENTITY_CONFLICT

SUPERVISORY_CONTINUITY_IDENTITY_CONFLICT

SUPERVISORY_CYCLE_MISMATCH

REALIZATION_IDENTITY_MISMATCH

COMMITMENT_IDENTITY_CONFLICT

REGISTRY_SNAPSHOT_CONFLICT

SCHEMA_VERSION_CONFLICT

CROSS_CYCLE_EVIDENCE

DUPLICATE_COMMITTED_IDENTITY

UNRESOLVABLE_BINDING

Upon failure:

realization certification SHALL terminate;
no historical certification SHALL occur;
no provenance construction SHALL begin.
Constitutional Output

Successful completion SHALL produce exactly one:

Validated Realization Evidence Binding

The Validated Realization Evidence Binding SHALL include:

realization-certification context;
validated supervisory cycle identity;
validated realization identity;
validated commitment identities;
validated registry references;
validated schema references;
binding status;
validation timestamp.

This artifact is an internal constitutional working artifact.

It SHALL NOT constitute a committed historical record.

Architectural Constraints

Evidence Identity and Binding Validation is an identity authority.

It SHALL NOT:

certify historical truth;
determine admissibility;
evaluate evidence quality;
construct historical records;
construct lineage;
perform replay;
perform audit.

Its sole responsibility is establishing one constitutionally valid realization-certification context.

Fundamental Binding Principle

The defining principle of this section is:

Only committed constitutional artifacts that resolve to one unique supervisory realization context may proceed to historical certification. Contract 011 SHALL reject conflicting, ambiguous, duplicated, or cross-cycle evidence rather than reconstruct or reinterpret constitutional identity.
