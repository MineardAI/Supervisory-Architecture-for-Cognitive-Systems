# SACS Contract 012 — External Verification Profile and Evidence Interface

## Section 5 — Relationship to Contract 011 and NullBlade

### Purpose

Section 5 defines the exact constitutional relationship between:

* Contract 011;
* Contract 012;
* the authorized external verification authority;
* the verification-status consumers that follow.

This section establishes the complete boundary chain from committed supervisory history to externally issued verification status.

Its purpose is to ensure that:

* Contract 011 remains the sole authority for committed supervisory history;
* Contract 012 remains the sole SACS authority for verification preparation and registration;
* NullBlade remains the external authority for verification judgment;
* no downstream result modifies the upstream historical record;
* every verification result remains bound to the exact submission from which it was produced.

---

# Constitutional Sequence

The canonical sequence is:

```text
Contract 011
Supervisory Cycle Commitment

↓

Committed Supervisory Cycle Record

↓

Contract 012
Verification Preparation and Submission Commitment

↓

Committed Verification Submission

↓

NullBlade
External Verification

↓

External Verification Result

↓

Contract 012
Verification Result Registration

↓

Committed Verification Status Record
```

Each transition SHALL preserve:

* artifact identity;
* authority identity;
* provenance;
* scope;
* version binding;
* commitment status;
* deterministic traceability.

No stage SHALL silently absorb the authority of another.

---

# Contract 011 as Historical Authority

Contract 011 is the terminal authority for one completed supervisory cycle.

Its canonical success artifact is:

```text
Committed Supervisory Cycle Record
```

That record establishes the immutable constitutional history of the cycle.

It SHALL contain or resolve the authoritative lineage necessary to determine:

* what supervisory state was committed;
* which governance and objectives applied;
* which Operational Profile and Reference State applied;
* which Control Command was committed;
* which Translation Transaction was committed;
* which Execution Disposition was committed;
* what realization occurred;
* what provenance and audit records were committed;
* whether the cycle completed successfully or through an explicitly committed failure path.

Contract 012 SHALL treat this record as authoritative and immutable.

---

# Contract 011 Completion Precondition

Contract 012 SHALL NOT begin canonical verification preparation for a supervisory cycle unless Contract 011 has reached a terminal committed state.

The required upstream condition is exactly one of:

```text
Committed Supervisory Cycle Record
```

or, where the Verification Profile expressly permits verification of a committed failure path:

```text
Committed Supervisory Cycle Failure Record
```

An incomplete, proposed, constructed, pending, or partially committed cycle record SHALL NOT qualify as canonical historical input.

Contract 012 MAY report the absence of a terminal Contract 011 artifact.

It SHALL NOT compensate for that absence.

---

# Historical Consumption Rule

Contract 012 consumes Contract 011 history read-only.

Read-only consumption means Contract 012 MAY:

* resolve artifact identities;
* verify hashes;
* follow committed lineage;
* extract profile-authorized evidence;
* bind claims to historical facts;
* construct evidence manifests;
* identify missing or conflicting evidence;
* preserve historical references.

Contract 012 SHALL NOT:

* insert new historical facts;
* remove historical facts;
* repair provenance;
* replace failed artifacts;
* alter timestamps;
* recalculate committed outcomes;
* reinterpret commitment meaning;
* generate substitute history.

The historical record remains unchanged regardless of whether verification succeeds, fails, or remains inconclusive.

---

# Contract 011 Artifacts as Evidence

A Committed Supervisory Cycle Record is the primary verification subject for whole-cycle claims.

Its constituent committed artifacts MAY become evidence where required by the applicable Verification Profile.

Such artifacts MAY include:

* Committed Observation Batch references;
* Committed Operational State Vector;
* Committed Governance Snapshot;
* Committed Objective Set;
* Committed Operational Profile;
* Committed Reference State;
* Committed Control Error;
* Committed Control Command;
* Committed Translation Transaction;
* Committed Execution Disposition;
* Committed Realization Record;
* Committed Supervisory Continuity Record;
* replay records;
* audit records;
* commitment records;
* failure records;
* registry snapshots;
* configuration snapshots;
* integrity proofs.

The existence of an artifact within the historical lineage does not automatically make it admissible for every verification claim.

Admissibility remains governed by the Verification Profile.

---

# Contract 011 Replay Relationship

Contract 011 MAY provide deterministic replay artifacts or replay results.

Contract 012 MAY include them as evidence where authorized.

The distinction SHALL remain:

```text
Committed Historical Artifact
≠
Replay Artifact
```

Replay may demonstrate reproducibility.

Replay does not replace original history.

A replay mismatch MAY become evidence relevant to a verification claim.

It SHALL NOT rewrite the committed cycle record.

---

# Contract 011 Audit Relationship

Contract 011 MAY provide audit representations concerning the supervisory cycle.

Contract 012 MAY include audit records as evidence where authorized.

The distinction SHALL remain:

```text
Audit Representation
≠
Committed Supervisory History
```

Audit may explain, inspect, or test historical consistency.

Audit does not become the source of historical authority.

Contract 012 SHALL preserve the exact identity and role of each audit artifact.

---

# Contract 012 as Verification Interface Authority

Contract 012 begins only after receiving an eligible committed subject.

Its constitutional authority is limited to:

* verification applicability;
* Verification Profile binding;
* claim binding;
* evidence resolution;
* evidence-package construction;
* evidence completeness evaluation;
* evidence integrity evaluation;
* submission commitment;
* external handoff;
* result intake;
* verification-status registration.

Contract 012 SHALL not decide whether the submitted claim is true.

That authority remains external.

---

# NullBlade Constitutional Identity

NullBlade is the designated external verification authority for SACS unless another external authority is explicitly authorized through constitutional governance.

NullBlade SHALL remain external to the SACS supervisory authority chain.

Its purpose is to evaluate bounded verification claims without inheriting authority to:

* operate SACS;
* modify SACS history;
* alter SACS governance;
* amend SACS contracts;
* commit SACS supervisory artifacts;
* authorize execution;
* adopt implementation changes.

NullBlade verifies.

It does not supervise.

---

# Externality Requirement

For a verification result to qualify as externally issued, the verifier SHALL possess an authority identity separate from the SACS component that:

* produced the verification subject;
* assembled the supervisory history;
* constructed the verification submission;
* registered the verification result.

Externality MAY be established through:

* separate trust domain;
* separate credentials;
* separate signing authority;
* separate execution environment;
* separate organizational authority;
* separately governed verification service;
* cryptographically distinct issuer identity.

Technical co-location alone does not necessarily invalidate externality.

However, constitutional authority, credentials, records, and decision control SHALL remain independently distinguishable.

---

# NullBlade Input

The canonical NullBlade input is:

```text
Committed Verification Submission
```

NullBlade SHALL NOT be expected to infer canonical verification scope from:

* an uncommitted evidence package;
* an informal request;
* a historical artifact supplied without a submission manifest;
* an operator description;
* an incomplete claim;
* an unbound evidence collection;
* ambient SACS state.

The submission SHALL provide the complete constitutional context required for the verification task.

---

# Submission Identity

Every Committed Verification Submission SHALL possess a globally unique and immutable identity.

That identity SHALL bind:

```text
verification_submission_id
verification_configuration_id
verification_profile_id
verification_profile_version
verification_requirement_set_id
verification_subject_id
verification_subject_type
claim_set_hash
evidence_package_id
evidence_manifest_hash
authorized_verifier_id
submission_scope
submission_timestamp
submission_commitment_id
submission_hash
```

NullBlade SHALL return the exact `verification_submission_id` in its result.

A result lacking a resolvable submission identity SHALL NOT be registered as canonical.

---

# NullBlade Claim Boundary

NullBlade SHALL evaluate only the claims included in the committed submission.

It MAY:

* verify a claim;
* reject a claim;
* report a claim as inconclusive;
* report insufficient evidence;
* report unsupported method requirements;
* narrow a result;
* attach limitations;
* attach exclusions;
* report verifier failure.

NullBlade SHALL NOT silently add new claims to the canonical result.

Additional observations MAY be returned separately.

They SHALL NOT become canonical verification claims unless later authorized and submitted through a new Contract 012 operation.

---

# Verification Method Relationship

Contract 012 identifies:

* required methods;
* permitted methods;
* prohibited methods;
* evidence dependencies;
* reporting requirements.

NullBlade determines:

* which permitted methods it actually performs;
* whether those methods satisfy the verification requirements;
* what findings arise;
* whether the claim is verified, rejected, limited, or inconclusive.

Contract 012 SHALL not report a method as performed unless NullBlade reports it as performed and the result passes authenticity validation.

---

# External Verification Result

NullBlade SHALL produce an `External Verification Result`.

The result SHALL contain at minimum:

```text
external_verification_result_id
verification_submission_id
verifier_id
verifier_version
issuer_identity
issuer_signature_or_integrity_proof
result_timestamp
claim_results
methods_performed
evidence_consumed
evidence_rejected
limitations
exclusions
uncertainties
validity_scope
validity_interval, where applicable
result_status
result_hash
```

Each claim result SHALL remain independently identifiable.

---

# Claim Result Classes

A claim result SHALL use one registered result class.

Permitted classes MAY include:

```text
VERIFIED
VERIFIED_WITH_LIMITATIONS
NOT_VERIFIED
REJECTED
INCONCLUSIVE
INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE
UNSUPPORTED_METHOD
OUT_OF_SCOPE
VERIFIER_FAILURE
WITHDRAWN
EXPIRED
```

The exact result vocabulary SHALL be defined by the applicable Verification Profile or result schema.

Contract 012 SHALL not translate an unfavorable or inconclusive class into a favorable class.

---

# Result Authenticity

Before registration, Contract 012 SHALL validate:

* recognized verifier identity;
* result signature or integrity proof;
* submission identity;
* claim identities;
* profile identity;
* result schema;
* result hash;
* declared result scope;
* timestamp validity;
* method reporting;
* limitation reporting.

Authenticity validation establishes that the result was issued by the recognized verifier and corresponds to the committed submission.

It does not establish that the result is substantively correct.

---

# Result Scope Preservation

The registered result SHALL not exceed:

* the submitted subject scope;
* the submitted claim scope;
* the submitted temporal scope;
* the submitted version scope;
* the submitted implementation scope;
* the submitted environment scope.

Where NullBlade returns a narrower scope, Contract 012 SHALL preserve the narrower scope.

Where NullBlade appears to return a broader scope, Contract 012 SHALL:

* reject the excess scope;
* register only the valid bounded portion where the profile permits partial registration; or
* fail result registration.

It SHALL not grant authority to the excess scope.

---

# Result Registration

After successful validation, Contract 012 SHALL construct:

```text
Committed Verification Status Record
```

The record SHALL preserve:

* submission identity;
* external result identity;
* verifier identity;
* claim-level results;
* verification methods;
* limitations;
* exclusions;
* validity scope;
* validity interval;
* result provenance;
* registration timestamp;
* registration commitment identity.

The record SHALL not rewrite the external result.

---

# Verification Status and Historical Status

Verification status is separate from historical status.

Formally:

```text
Historical Commitment Status
≠
External Verification Status
```

A cycle may remain historically committed while a claim concerning that cycle is:

* verified;
* not verified;
* inconclusive;
* unsupported;
* expired.

External verification status does not change whether the cycle occurred or was historically committed.

---

# Negative Verification Results

A negative result SHALL be preserved with the same integrity as a positive result.

Contract 012 SHALL NOT:

* delete a negative result;
* conceal a negative result;
* retry without preserving the prior result;
* replace a negative result with a later favorable result;
* mark a claim unverified only internally while publishing it as verified.

Multiple results MAY exist for different submissions or verification attempts.

Each SHALL retain its own immutable identity and lineage.

---

# Reverification

A new verification attempt SHALL require a new Committed Verification Submission where any of the following changes:

* claim;
* evidence package;
* Verification Profile;
* Verification Requirement Set;
* verifier;
* method requirements;
* subject version;
* implementation version;
* environment;
* scope.

A prior submission SHALL not be mutated for reverification.

The relationship SHALL be represented explicitly:

```text
Prior Verification Submission
        ↓
Reverification Relationship
        ↓
New Verification Submission
```

---

# Result Supersession

A later result MAY supersede an earlier result only where the applicable Verification Profile permits supersession.

Supersession SHALL NOT erase the prior result.

It SHALL identify:

```text
superseding_result_id
superseded_result_id
supersession_basis
scope_relationship
effective_time
```

A later result with different scope does not automatically supersede an earlier result.

---

# Verification Expiration

A verification result MAY expire where validity depends upon:

* time;
* implementation version;
* environment;
* profile version;
* evidence currency;
* verifier policy;
* cryptographic validity;
* deployment state.

Expiration SHALL modify only the current registered verification status.

It SHALL not modify the original external result.

The historical fact that the result was issued remains immutable.

---

# NullBlade Unavailability

Where NullBlade is unavailable, Contract 012 MAY:

* preserve a committed pending submission;
* retry handoff according to committed retry rules;
* record transmission failure;
* record acknowledgment failure;
* register verifier unavailability.

Contract 012 SHALL NOT:

* perform the verification itself;
* substitute an unauthorized verifier;
* declare the claim verified by timeout;
* infer a result from prior submissions;
* discard the committed submission.

---

# Unauthorized Verifier Result

A result issued by an unrecognized verifier SHALL NOT become a canonical Committed Verification Status Record.

It MAY be retained as non-authoritative external material where policy permits.

Such retention SHALL clearly indicate:

```text
UNAUTHORIZED_VERIFIER_RESULT
```

No consumer SHALL treat it as Contract 012 verification authority.

---

# Disagreement Between SACS and NullBlade

Contract 012 SHALL not override NullBlade because an internal SACS check disagrees with the external result.

Where disagreement exists:

* the internal check SHALL remain an internal conformance record;
* the external result SHALL remain the external verification result;
* the disagreement SHALL be preserved;
* a new verification submission MAY be authorized;
* human governance MAY review the conflict.

Contract 012 SHALL not resolve the disagreement by rewriting either record.

---

# Human Governance Relationship

Human governance determines:

* whether a verifier is authorized;
* whether a Verification Profile is adopted;
* whether a result is sufficient for deployment or certification;
* whether a negative result requires action;
* whether reverification is required;
* whether a contract or implementation should change.

Contract 012 provides committed verification evidence and status.

It does not make the governance decision.

---

# Complete Authority Chain

The complete authority chain is:

| Constitutional Function                         | Authority         |
| ----------------------------------------------- | ----------------- |
| Supervisory operation                           | Contracts 001–009 |
| Historical certification                        | Contract 010      |
| Supervisory-cycle assembly and commitment       | Contract 011      |
| Verification preparation                        | Contract 012      |
| Verification-submission commitment              | Contract 012      |
| External verification judgment                  | NullBlade         |
| Verification-result registration                | Contract 012      |
| Deployment, adoption, amendment, or remediation | Human Governance  |

This allocation SHALL remain explicit in every conforming implementation.

---

# Failure Conditions

The Contract 011-to-Contract 012 boundary SHALL fail when:

* no terminal committed cycle artifact exists;
* multiple conflicting terminal cycle artifacts exist;
* the subject identity cannot be resolved;
* required lineage is unavailable;
* the subject hash is invalid;
* the subject has been altered after commitment;
* the requested claim exceeds the committed history.

The Contract 012-to-NullBlade boundary SHALL fail when:

* no committed submission exists;
* verifier identity is unresolved;
* verifier authorization is absent;
* transmission integrity cannot be established;
* submission acknowledgment conflicts with the committed identity.

The NullBlade-to-Contract 012 boundary SHALL fail when:

* result issuer identity is invalid;
* the result signature is invalid;
* submission identity is missing or conflicting;
* claim identity is unknown;
* result scope exceeds permitted scope;
* mandatory result fields are absent;
* the result has been altered.

Every failure SHALL be explicitly represented.

---

# Prohibitions

Contract 012 SHALL NOT:

* begin canonical verification from incomplete Contract 011 history;
* treat Contract 011 audit as NullBlade verification;
* allow NullBlade to modify Contract 011 history;
* send uncommitted evidence as a canonical submission;
* accept a result that cannot identify its submission;
* register results from unauthorized verifiers;
* broaden a NullBlade result;
* hide negative results;
* erase superseded results;
* substitute internal replay for external reverification;
* infer governance approval from verification status.

---

# Section 5 Invariants

> **Contract 011 SHALL remain the sole authority for immutable supervisory-cycle history; Contract 012 SHALL remain the sole SACS authority for verification preparation, submission commitment, and result registration; and NullBlade SHALL remain the separate external authority that issues the verification judgment.**

> **Every External Verification Result SHALL resolve to exactly one Committed Verification Submission, and every Committed Verification Status Record SHALL preserve that submission, the verifier-issued result, its exact scope, and its complete provenance without modifying the underlying supervisory history.**
