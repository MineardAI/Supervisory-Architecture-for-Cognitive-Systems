# SACS Contract 012 — External Verification Profile and Evidence Interface

## Section 23 — Architectural Completion and Successor Contracts

### Purpose

Section 23 concludes Contract 012 by identifying the constitutional boundaries established by the contract and defining the relationship between Contract 012 and any successor constitutional contracts.

Unlike the preceding sections, this section introduces no new constitutional authority, artifacts, commitments, or processing stages.

Its sole purpose is to define the constitutional completion of External Verification and establish the architectural transition to future constitutional capabilities.

---

# Constitutional Completion

Contract 012 is constitutionally complete upon the successful commitment of a Committed Verification Exchange Record or a Committed Verification Exchange Failure.

At that point:

* verification preparation has concluded;
* submission has concluded;
* constitutional custody has concluded;
* external verification has concluded;
* admission has concluded;
* certification has concluded;
* exchange history has been committed;
* replay and audit capabilities have been established.

No further constitutional processing belongs to Contract 012.

---

# Constitutional Boundary

Contract 012 governs only the constitutional exchange of trust between SACS and independent verification authorities.

Contract 012 SHALL NOT govern:

* supervisory decision making;
* policy evolution;
* constitutional learning;
* institutional knowledge management;
* governance modification;
* operational adaptation.

Those responsibilities belong to successor constitutional contracts, if defined.

---

# Successor Relationship

Any successor contract SHALL consume only committed historical artifacts produced by Contract 012.

Successor contracts SHALL NOT consume:

* partially constructed artifacts;
* pre-commitment artifacts;
* transient processing state;
* replay artifacts;
* audit representations.

The canonical output of Contract 012 is therefore:

```text
Committed Verification Exchange Record
```

or

```text
Committed Verification Exchange Failure
```

These artifacts constitute the exclusive constitutional interface to subsequent contracts.

---

# Constitutional Independence

No successor contract SHALL reinterpret or modify the constitutional meaning of any artifact committed under Contract 012.

Successor contracts MAY:

* analyze;
* compare;
* aggregate;
* summarize;
* learn from;
* reference;

the committed history produced by Contract 012.

They SHALL NOT alter that history.

---

# Architectural Completion Statement

With the completion of Contract 012, SACS establishes a complete constitutional architecture for inter-constitutional verification.

The architecture now supports:

* deterministic supervisory execution;
* immutable historical preservation;
* sovereign external verification;
* bounded constitutional trust exchange;
* deterministic replay;
* independent audit;
* permanent constitutional traceability.

Future constitutional contracts extend these capabilities but SHALL NOT redefine them.

Contract 012 therefore concludes as the authoritative constitutional specification governing External Verification within the SACS architecture.
