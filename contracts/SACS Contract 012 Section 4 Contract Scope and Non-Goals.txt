# SACS Contract 012 — External Verification Profile and Evidence Interface

## Section 4 — Contract Scope and Non-Goals

### Purpose

Section 4 defines the complete functional scope of Contract 012 and establishes the activities that remain constitutionally outside its authority.

This section prevents Contract 012 from expanding into:

* supervisory operation;
* historical certification;
* internal audit;
* implementation approval;
* regulatory certification;
* constitutional interpretation;
* constitutional amendment;
* autonomous institutional learning.

Contract 012 exists only to transform an eligible committed verification subject into a bounded, immutable external verification submission and to register the result returned by an authorized external verifier.

---

# Constitutional Scope

Contract 012 owns the following constitutional functions:

1. verification-purpose declaration;
2. verification applicability evaluation;
3. Verification Profile definition and resolution;
4. Verification Requirement Set definition and resolution;
5. verification method definition and authorization;
6. verification subject binding;
7. claim definition and identity binding;
8. evidence requirement resolution;
9. evidence admissibility evaluation;
10. evidence completeness evaluation;
11. evidence integrity evaluation;
12. Verification Evidence Package construction;
13. Verification Submission construction;
14. Verification Submission commitment;
15. external verification handoff;
16. External Verification Result intake;
17. result authenticity and identity validation;
18. verification-status registration;
19. verification lineage preservation;
20. deterministic replay of Contract 012 preparation and registration operations.

These functions define the complete constitutional scope of Contract 012.

No implementation SHALL extend Contract 012 beyond them without a future constitutional contract or explicit amendment to the SACS architecture.

---

# Core Transformation Scope

The primary Contract 012 transformation is:

```text
Eligible Committed Verification Subject
+
Committed Verification Configuration
+
Authorized Verification Claim Set
+
Resolved Verification Evidence
=
Constructed Verification Evidence Package
```

followed by:

```text
Constructed Verification Evidence Package
+
Evidence Completeness Result
+
Evidence Integrity Result
+
Authorized External Verifier Binding
=
Committed Verification Submission
```

and subsequently:

```text
Committed Verification Submission
+
Authentic External Verification Result
=
Committed Verification Status Record
```

Each transformation SHALL remain separately identifiable.

Each transformation SHALL preserve its own:

* input identity;
* output identity;
* authority boundary;
* commitment boundary;
* provenance;
* failure representation.

---

# Verification Preparation Scope

Verification preparation includes only the deterministic assembly of the materials required for an external verifier to evaluate one or more explicit claims.

Preparation MAY include:

* resolving committed artifacts;
* locating authorized evidence;
* validating evidence identities;
* validating evidence lineage;
* validating evidence schemas;
* confirming required versions;
* constructing deterministic evidence manifests;
* calculating hashes;
* constructing claim-to-evidence mappings;
* identifying missing evidence;
* identifying inadmissible evidence;
* binding the submission to one external verifier;
* committing the final submission.

Preparation SHALL NOT include deciding whether the claim is true.

---

# Verification Registration Scope

After an External Verification Result is returned, Contract 012 MAY:

* validate issuer identity;
* validate signature or integrity proof;
* validate submission identity;
* validate claim identity;
* validate scope consistency;
* validate result schema;
* validate method reporting;
* validate limitation reporting;
* validate result completeness;
* register the result;
* construct a Committed Verification Status Record.

Registration SHALL preserve the result as issued.

Contract 012 SHALL NOT improve, soften, strengthen, reinterpret, or override the result.

---

# Canonical Input Scope

Contract 012 MAY consume only committed or explicitly admissible evidence artifacts.

Its primary canonical input is:

```text
Committed Supervisory Cycle Record
```

Additional canonical inputs MAY include committed artifacts referenced by that record or explicitly authorized by a Verification Profile.

Contract 012 MAY also consume external evidence where the applicable Verification Profile expressly permits it and where the evidence satisfies the required admissibility and integrity conditions.

Contract 012 SHALL NOT consume an uncommitted artifact as authoritative evidence.

---

# Canonical Output Scope

The canonical success outputs of Contract 012 are:

```text
Committed Verification Submission
```

and, after external verification:

```text
Committed Verification Status Record
```

These artifacts have different constitutional meanings.

The `Committed Verification Submission` establishes:

* what was submitted;
* which claims were submitted;
* which evidence was included;
* which verifier was authorized;
* which scope applied;
* which methods were requested or permitted.

The `Committed Verification Status Record` establishes:

* which result was received;
* who issued it;
* which submission it concerns;
* which claims were verified, rejected, limited, or unresolved;
* which methods were reported;
* which limitations apply;
* whether the result remains valid within its declared scope.

Neither artifact modifies the verification subject.

---

# Failure Output Scope

Contract 012 SHALL explicitly represent failure.

Failure MAY occur during:

* applicability evaluation;
* profile resolution;
* configuration binding;
* subject binding;
* claim binding;
* evidence resolution;
* admissibility evaluation;
* completeness evaluation;
* integrity evaluation;
* submission construction;
* submission commitment;
* external handoff;
* result intake;
* issuer validation;
* result validation;
* status registration.

A failure occurring before submission commitment SHALL NOT produce a Committed Verification Submission.

A failure occurring after submission commitment SHALL NOT erase or invalidate the committed submission.

A result-intake or registration failure SHALL preserve the fact that a submission was committed and handed off.

---

# Verification Profile Scope

Contract 012 defines the constitutional structure of Verification Profiles.

A Verification Profile MAY specify:

* supported verification purposes;
* eligible subject classes;
* eligible claim classes;
* required evidence classes;
* optional evidence classes;
* prohibited evidence classes;
* admissibility rules;
* completeness rules;
* integrity rules;
* required verification methods;
* permitted verification methods;
* external verifier requirements;
* result schema requirements;
* validity rules;
* expiration rules;
* replay requirements;
* conformance tests.

A Verification Profile SHALL NOT:

* declare a claim verified;
* modify the subject;
* authorize execution;
* substitute for governance;
* approve an implementation;
* amend a SACS contract.

---

# Verification Requirement Scope

A Verification Requirement Set defines what must be satisfied for a claim to be prepared and submitted under a Verification Profile.

Requirements MAY address:

* evidence presence;
* evidence provenance;
* evidence integrity;
* claim specificity;
* method coverage;
* temporal coverage;
* version binding;
* implementation binding;
* environmental binding;
* lineage closure;
* authority preservation;
* replayability;
* external verifier capability.

Verification requirements govern submission preparation.

They do not predetermine the external verdict.

---

# Evidence Scope

Contract 012 owns evidence selection only within the bounds established by the committed Verification Configuration.

It MAY determine whether a candidate artifact:

* matches a required evidence class;
* belongs to the subject lineage;
* satisfies the applicable schema;
* satisfies admissibility requirements;
* satisfies integrity requirements;
* falls within the declared scope;
* is permitted by the Verification Profile.

It SHALL NOT determine:

* whether favorable evidence is more persuasive than unfavorable evidence;
* whether an inconvenient artifact should be excluded;
* whether a failed test should be omitted;
* whether a missing artifact may be reconstructed;
* whether a contradictory artifact may be ignored.

Evidence selection SHALL be rule-bound, not outcome-seeking.

---

# External Handoff Scope

Contract 012 owns the constitutional handoff of one Committed Verification Submission to one authorized external verification authority.

The handoff MAY include:

* submission transmission;
* verifier identity binding;
* submission receipt confirmation;
* transmission integrity confirmation;
* handoff timestamp registration;
* external job identity registration;
* retry state registration;
* acknowledgment preservation.

Contract 012 SHALL NOT control how the external verifier conducts its analysis unless a Verification Profile defines mandatory method constraints.

Even where method constraints exist, Contract 012 validates reported conformance but does not perform the external verification itself.

---

# Replay Scope

Contract 012 SHALL support deterministic replay of:

* applicability evaluation;
* profile resolution;
* configuration binding;
* subject binding;
* evidence resolution;
* evidence-package construction;
* completeness evaluation;
* integrity evaluation;
* submission construction;
* result-registration validation.

Replay MAY establish whether Contract 012 performed its own functions correctly.

Replay SHALL NOT recreate the constitutional authority of an external verifier.

An external verdict SHALL NOT be regenerated internally and treated as equivalent to the original external result.

Formally:

```text
Contract 012 Replay
≠
External Reverification
```

---

# Audit Scope

Contract 012 SHALL expose sufficient records for audit of:

* profile selection;
* requirement resolution;
* evidence inclusion;
* evidence exclusion;
* completeness evaluation;
* integrity evaluation;
* submission commitment;
* external handoff;
* result intake;
* status registration.

Audit MAY determine whether Contract 012 complied with its constitutional duties.

Audit SHALL NOT:

* replace the external result;
* amend the external result;
* reinterpret the verification claim;
* modify the submission;
* modify the verification subject.

Audit remains observational.

---

# Conformance Scope

A Contract 012 implementation MAY be evaluated for conformance with this contract.

Conformance MAY include:

* schema conformance;
* deterministic behavior;
* commitment behavior;
* failure behavior;
* profile resolution;
* evidence completeness behavior;
* evidence integrity behavior;
* replay behavior;
* result-registration behavior;
* authority separation.

Contract 012 conformance SHALL NOT imply that every submission produced by the implementation is externally verified.

Implementation conformance and claim verification are separate.

---

# Non-Goal: Supervisory Operation

Contract 012 SHALL NOT:

* observe runtime telemetry for supervisory purposes;
* estimate operational state;
* select objectives;
* apply governance;
* select Operational Profiles;
* construct Reference States;
* compute control errors;
* generate Control Commands;
* translate Control Commands;
* adjudicate execution;
* certify realization;
* commit supervisory history.

Those functions belong to Contracts 001 through 011.

---

# Non-Goal: Historical Reconstruction

Contract 012 SHALL NOT reconstruct, repair, or supplement constitutional history.

Where required historical evidence is absent, Contract 012 SHALL report:

```text
REQUIRED_EVIDENCE_UNAVAILABLE
```

It SHALL NOT fabricate a replacement.

A replay output MAY be included only where explicitly permitted and clearly identified as replay evidence.

Replay evidence SHALL never be mislabeled as original history.

---

# Non-Goal: Internal Self-Verification

SACS SHALL NOT issue its own external verification verdict through Contract 012.

Internal checks MAY establish:

* preparation correctness;
* package completeness;
* package integrity;
* profile conformance;
* submission validity.

They SHALL NOT establish independent external assurance.

The system that produced the artifact SHALL NOT become the sole constitutional authority declaring that artifact externally verified.

---

# Non-Goal: Implementation Certification

Contract 012 SHALL NOT issue:

* product certification;
* deployment certification;
* safety certification;
* security certification;
* regulatory certification;
* legal certification;
* standards accreditation;
* commercial approval.

An external verifier MAY issue a result relevant to such activities.

Human institutions determine whether that result satisfies any certification or approval process.

---

# Non-Goal: Governance Replacement

Contract 012 SHALL NOT decide whether:

* a system should be deployed;
* a system should continue operating;
* a failed verification requires shutdown;
* a limitation is acceptable;
* a risk should be accepted;
* a contract should be amended;
* a control strategy should change.

Those decisions belong to authorized governance.

Contract 012 reports verification status.

It does not govern the response to that status.

---

# Non-Goal: Constitutional Interpretation

Contract 012 SHALL NOT create new constitutional meaning by interpreting ambiguous contracts during verification preparation.

Where contract meaning is unresolved, Contract 012 SHALL produce an explicit ambiguity or applicability failure.

It SHALL NOT silently select the interpretation most favorable to submission success.

Constitutional ambiguity requires constitutional resolution outside Contract 012.

---

# Non-Goal: Constitutional Amendment

Contract 012 SHALL NOT:

* change a contract;
* change an invariant;
* change an authority boundary;
* create new artifact authority;
* redefine a canonical term;
* weaken a requirement;
* waive a mandatory failure;
* authorize a new external verifier without the required governance process.

Verification evidence MAY reveal the need for amendment.

It SHALL NOT perform that amendment.

---

# Non-Goal: Supervisory Learning

Contract 012 SHALL NOT convert verification history into autonomous policy changes, contract revisions, control changes, or institutional learning.

It MAY preserve verification results for later authorized consumers.

It SHALL NOT:

* infer new policy;
* adopt recommendations;
* modify profiles;
* change requirements;
* update controllers;
* alter governance;
* change future supervisory behavior.

Any constitutional learning or improvement authority SHALL exist in a separate downstream module or contract.

---

# Non-Goal: Evidence Advocacy

Contract 012 SHALL not act as an advocate for a favorable verification outcome.

It SHALL NOT:

* omit unfavorable evidence;
* prioritize favorable evidence;
* rewrite claim language to avoid failure;
* suppress limitations;
* conceal uncertainty;
* select weaker verification methods to improve pass probability;
* retry verification until a favorable result appears without preserving prior results;
* present an inconclusive result as verified.

Contract 012 prepares evidence.

It does not argue the case.

---

# Non-Goal: Universal Assurance

No Contract 012 artifact SHALL be interpreted as proof of:

* universal safety;
* universal correctness;
* universal reliability;
* universal alignment;
* perpetual validity;
* absence of all failure modes;
* fitness for every environment;
* compliance with every law or standard.

Every verification result remains bounded by its exact claims and scope.

---

# Prohibited Authority Collapse

The following combinations are constitutionally prohibited:

```text
History Producer + External Verifier
```

```text
Submission Constructor + Verdict Issuer
```

```text
Verdict Issuer + Constitutional Adopter
```

```text
Evidence Resolver + Outcome Advocate
```

```text
Verification Registrar + Result Reinterpreter
```

An implementation MAY technically host multiple components.

Technical co-location SHALL NOT collapse their constitutional identities, credentials, records, or authorities.

---

# Section 4 Invariant

> **Contract 012 SHALL remain limited to verification applicability, profile and requirement binding, evidence preparation, submission commitment, external-verifier handoff, result intake, and verification-status registration. It SHALL NOT operate the supervisory system, modify history, verify its own claims, certify implementations, replace governance, amend the constitution, or convert verification results into autonomous institutional change.**
