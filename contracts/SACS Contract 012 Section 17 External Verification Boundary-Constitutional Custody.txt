SACS Contract 012 — External Verification Profile and Evidence Interface
Section 17 — External Verification Boundary and Constitutional Custody
Purpose

Section 17 establishes the constitutional boundary governing the transfer of a committed Verification Submission from SACS constitutional custody to an authorized external verification authority.

Its purpose is to preserve constitutional identity while permitting an independent constitutional authority to evaluate submitted verification claims.

This section governs:

constitutional boundary crossing;
custody transition;
delegated verification responsibility;
authority preservation;
ownership preservation;
identity preservation;
traceability;
constitutional sovereignty.

This section does not define:

network transport;
communication protocols;
API design;
serialization formats;
verifier algorithms; or
verification judgments.

Those belong to implementation or to the external verifier.

Constitutional Question

How may a constitutional artifact cross an external authority boundary while preserving identity, ownership, authority, custody, provenance, and constitutional sovereignty?

Section 17 establishes the constitutional conditions under which a Committed Verification Submission may leave SACS constitutional custody without transferring constitutional authority.

Constitutional Identity

The defining constitutional identity of the External Verification Boundary is:

A constitutional boundary permits delegated verification responsibility while preserving the originating constitutional system's ownership, authority, historical sovereignty, and artifact identity.

Crossing the boundary authorizes evaluation.

It does not authorize modification.

It does not authorize reinterpretation.

It does not authorize constitutional control.

Constitutional Custody

Contract 012 introduces the constitutional concept of custody.

Constitutional custody identifies which constitutional system is presently responsible for possessing, protecting, transmitting, or evaluating an artifact without becoming its constitutional owner.

Custody is distinct from:

Custody is temporary.

Ownership is permanent.

Authority remains constitutional.

The Four Constitutional Properties

For every Committed Verification Submission crossing the external boundary, the following properties SHALL remain distinct.

Ownership

The Committed Verification Submission SHALL remain permanently owned by SACS.

Ownership SHALL never transfer.

Historical Sovereignty

The submission SHALL remain part of immutable SACS constitutional history.

External systems SHALL NOT modify that history.

Verification Responsibility

Responsibility for evaluating submitted Verification Claims MAY be delegated to the authorized external verifier.

Delegation SHALL remain limited to verification evaluation.

Constitutional Custody

Custody of the submitted artifact MAY temporarily reside with the authorized verifier for the duration of the verification operation.

Custody SHALL terminate upon completion, cancellation, expiration, or withdrawal of the verification operation.

Delegation Doctrine

The External Verification Boundary SHALL preserve the following constitutional doctrine:

Verification responsibility may be delegated across constitutional boundaries. Constitutional authority shall remain within its originating constitutional system.

Delegation SHALL NOT:

broaden authority;
duplicate authority;
surrender authority;
create shared constitutional ownership;
create shared commitment authority.

Delegation SHALL remain bounded to the verification operation.

Boundary Preconditions

A Committed Verification Submission SHALL cross the constitutional boundary only when all of the following exist:

one Committed Verification Submission;
one Verification Submission Commitment Record;
one authorized verifier identity;
one applicable Verification Profile;
one applicable Verification Requirement Set;
one complete Submission Manifest;
complete integrity bindings;
complete provenance bindings;
complete constitutional lineage; and
no unresolved constitutional prohibition.

Failure of any mandatory precondition SHALL prohibit boundary crossing.

Authorized External Verifier

The receiving constitutional authority SHALL possess:

verified constitutional identity;
authorized verifier credentials;
compatible verifier capabilities;
recognized trust-domain identity;
valid constitutional authorization; and
applicable Verification Profile compatibility.

An unauthorized verifier SHALL NOT receive constitutional custody.

Custody Transfer

Boundary crossing SHALL establish one explicit Custody Transfer Record.

The record SHALL identify:

custody_transfer_id
verification_submission_id
originating_constitution
receiving_constitution
originating_custodian
receiving_custodian
authorized_verifier_identity
custody_start_time
custody_reason
custody_scope
custody_constraints
custody_hash

Custody Transfer SHALL NOT modify the Verification Submission.

Boundary Integrity

The External Verification Boundary SHALL preserve:

submission identity;
submission hash;
claim identities;
evidence identities;
Submission Manifest;
Verification Profile reference;
Requirement Set reference;
constitutional lineage;
replay identity;
commitment identity.

No artifact SHALL be altered during constitutional transfer.

Boundary Constraints

The receiving constitutional authority SHALL NOT:

modify the Committed Verification Submission;
rewrite Verification Claims;
alter evidence;
broaden submitted scope;
weaken constitutional restrictions;
reinterpret committed lineage;
modify SACS historical records;
issue SACS commitments.

The verifier's authority SHALL remain limited to evaluating the submitted claims.

Boundary Failure

Boundary crossing SHALL fail upon detection of:

unauthorized verifier identity;
invalid verifier credentials;
integrity failure;
Submission Manifest inconsistency;
custody conflict;
constitutional identity mismatch;
unresolved trust-domain incompatibility;
prohibited delegation;
replay attack;
duplicate custody transfer.

Failure SHALL produce one explicit Verification Boundary Failure artifact.

No constitutional custody SHALL transfer following failure.

Boundary Replay

Replay of a boundary transfer SHALL reproduce:

identical custody identity;
identical submission identity;
identical verifier identity;
identical constitutional lineage;
identical integrity bindings;
identical custody constraints.

Replay SHALL verify constitutional consistency.

Replay SHALL NOT reproduce the external verification judgment.

Constitutional Invariants

For every Committed Verification Submission:

ownership SHALL remain with SACS;
historical sovereignty SHALL remain with SACS;
constitutional authority SHALL remain with its originating constitutional system;
only verification responsibility MAY be delegated;
custody SHALL remain explicit, bounded, and temporary;
no boundary crossing SHALL modify a committed artifact;
every custody transfer SHALL preserve identity, integrity, provenance, and lineage;
identical committed inputs SHALL produce identical constitutional custody records.
Constitutional Transition

Section 17 completes the constitutional transfer of custody between independent constitutional systems.

The Committed Verification Submission has now left SACS constitutional custody while remaining under SACS constitutional ownership and authority.
