SACS Contract 012 — External Verification Profile and Evidence Interface
Section 15 — Verification Submission Construction
Purpose

Section 15 establishes the constitutional process by which a validated Verification Evidence Package is transformed into the canonical Verification Submission.

Where Section 14 determines that a Verification Evidence Package is constitutionally complete and valid, this section constructs the immutable constitutional artifact that represents the complete request for external verification.

Verification Submission Construction is the final internal transformation performed by Contract 012.

It does not perform external verification.

It does not interpret verification results.

It does not communicate with external verification systems.

Its sole responsibility is to construct the canonical Verification Submission that may subsequently be committed and delivered across the constitutional boundary.

Constitutional Question

How is the validated Verification Evidence Package transformed into the canonical object delivered to the external verifier?

Verification Submission Construction SHALL determine:

submission identity;
submission authority;
verifier identity;
submission manifest;
claim inventory;
evidence inventory;
profile references;
Verification Requirement Set references;
Verification Method references;
package references;
integrity bindings;
provenance bindings;
submission metadata;
serialization identity;
constitutional lineage; and
cryptographic identity.

Only a successfully constructed Verification Submission MAY proceed to constitutional commitment.

Constitutional Identity

A Verification Submission is one immutable constitutional artifact that represents the complete, validated, authoritative request for external verification.

The Verification Submission SHALL contain every constitutional component required for an external verifier to evaluate the submitted verification claims without requiring additional constitutional interpretation.

A Verification Submission is:

complete;
immutable;
deterministic;
self-describing;
replayable;
transport-independent; and
constitutionally authoritative.

A Verification Submission is not:

an external verification request;
an implementation-specific protocol message;
a transport payload;
a verification result; or
authorization for an external verifier to modify constitutional history.
Constitutional Authority

Contract 012 SHALL exclusively own:

Verification Submission construction;
submission identity assignment;
submission manifest construction;
submission metadata construction;
verifier binding;
package referencing;
claim inventory construction;
evidence inventory construction;
integrity binding;
provenance binding;
serialization identity; and
construction of the Verification Submission.

Contract 012 SHALL NOT:

perform external verification;
modify verification claims;
modify evidence;
modify the Verification Evidence Package;
reinterpret constitutional authority;
communicate with external systems; or
authorize historical modification.
Construction Inputs

Verification Submission Construction SHALL consume only committed constitutional artifacts, including:

Verification Evidence Validation Report;
Verification Evidence Package;
Committed Verification Profile Binding;
Committed Verification Requirement Set;
Verification Method Definitions;
committed Verification Claims;
verifier identity;
submission metadata;
registry snapshots; and
applicable constitutional lineage.

No unvalidated package SHALL participate in Submission Construction.

Submission Identity

Every Verification Submission SHALL possess one immutable constitutional identity.

Submission identity SHALL include:

submission identifier;
submission version;
submission authority identifier;
verification subject identifier;
verifier identifier;
construction timestamp;
serialization version;
replay identity;
lineage identifier; and
cryptographic submission identity.

Submission identity SHALL remain immutable following successful construction.

Verifier Binding

Every Verification Submission SHALL identify exactly one constitutional verification destination.

Verifier binding SHALL specify:

verifier identity;
verifier class;
supported Verification Methods;
supported protocol version;
constitutional capability profile;
trust relationship;
communication constraints; and
verification compatibility.

Verifier binding identifies the intended verification authority.

It does not initiate communication.

Submission Manifest Construction

Every Verification Submission SHALL contain one canonical Submission Manifest.

The Submission Manifest SHALL enumerate:

submission identity;
verification subject;
Verification Claims;
Verification Profile;
Verification Requirement Set;
Verification Methods;
Verification Evidence Package;
evidence inventory;
integrity references;
provenance references;
constitutional lineage; and
replay identity.

The Submission Manifest SHALL constitute the authoritative inventory of submission contents.

Claim Inventory

The Verification Submission SHALL contain a complete inventory of every Verification Claim presented for external evaluation.

Claim inventory SHALL preserve:

claim identity;
claim version;
claim authority;
supported evidence references;
applicable Verification Methods;
profile applicability;
Requirement Set applicability; and
constitutional lineage.

Claims SHALL NOT be modified during Submission Construction.

Evidence Inventory

The Verification Submission SHALL reference the complete Verification Evidence Package.

Evidence inventory SHALL preserve:

package identity;
evidence references;
evidence definitions;
evidence classes;
manifest references;
integrity references;
provenance references; and
replay references.

Evidence SHALL be referenced rather than reconstructed.

Reference Binding

Submission Construction SHALL bind every constitutional dependency required for external verification.

Bindings SHALL include:

Verification Profile reference;
Verification Requirement Set reference;
Verification Method references;
Verification Evidence Package reference;
Verification Evidence Validation Report reference;
registry snapshot references;
constitutional artifact references; and
applicable constitutional lineage.

Every reference SHALL remain immutable.

Integrity Binding

Submission Construction SHALL establish complete submission integrity.

Integrity bindings SHALL include:

submission hash;
manifest hash;
package hash;
Validation Report hash;
claim inventory hash;
cryptographic signatures;
immutable identifiers; and
integrity relationships.

Integrity bindings preserve authenticity.

They do not establish verification success.

Provenance Binding

Every Verification Submission SHALL preserve complete constitutional provenance.

Provenance SHALL identify:

producing contract;
producing implementation;
producing authority;
producing constitutional artifacts;
producing registry snapshots;
commitment lineage;
replay lineage; and
historical lineage.

No component SHALL enter the submission without complete provenance.

Submission Metadata

Every Verification Submission SHALL contain canonical metadata describing the submission.

Submission metadata SHALL include:

submission identity;
verification subject;
verifier identity;
Verification Profile identity;
Verification Requirement Set identity;
Verification Method identities;
submission version;
serialization version;
replay version;
construction metadata;
constitutional authority;

Validation Report identity; and
lifecycle state.
Canonical Serialization

Every Verification Submission SHALL possess one canonical serialization.

Canonical serialization SHALL preserve:

deterministic encoding;
deterministic decoding;
deterministic hashing;
deterministic replay;
implementation independence; and
transport independence.

Equivalent constitutional submissions SHALL serialize identically.

Construction Rules

Verification Submission Construction SHALL verify construction completeness before producing the Verification Submission.

Construction SHALL ensure:

every required constitutional reference exists;
every required Validation Report exists;
every required Verification Claim is represented;
every required package reference exists;
every integrity binding exists;
every provenance binding exists;
verifier identity is complete;
no prohibited content exists; and
no unresolved constitutional dependency remains.

Submission Construction SHALL NOT evaluate verification claims.

Submission Construction SHALL NOT authorize external communication.

Canonical Verification Submission

Successful Submission Construction SHALL produce one immutable constitutional artifact containing:

submission identity;
Submission Manifest;
verifier binding;
Verification Claim inventory;
Verification Profile reference;
Verification Requirement Set reference;
Verification Method references;
Verification Evidence Package reference;
Verification Evidence Validation Report reference;
integrity bindings;
provenance bindings;
registry snapshot references;
constitutional lineage;
replay metadata;
serialization identity;
submission metadata; and
cryptographic identity.

This Verification Submission SHALL become the exclusive constitutional artifact eligible for commitment and external delivery.

Submission Construction Failure

Verification Submission Construction SHALL terminate immediately upon any constitutional construction failure, including but not limited to:

missing Validation Report;
missing Verification Evidence Package;
missing Verification Claims;
incomplete Submission Manifest;
unresolved constitutional references;
verifier incompatibility;
missing integrity bindings;
missing provenance;
serialization failure;
duplicate submission identity;
prohibited submission content; or
nondeterministic construction.

Submission Construction failure SHALL produce one explicit constitutional construction failure artifact.

No partial Verification Submission SHALL be produced.

Constitutional Invariants

For every successful Verification Evidence Validation Report:

exactly one Verification Submission SHALL be constructed;
every Verification Claim SHALL be represented exactly once;
every constitutional dependency SHALL be explicitly referenced;
every submission SHALL preserve constitutional identity, authority, provenance, integrity, lineage, and replay determinism;
no submission SHALL contain unauthorized, unresolved, or implementation-defined content;
Submission Construction SHALL not alter the meaning, authority, or structure of any Verification Claim or Verification Evidence; and
identical constitutional inputs SHALL always produce an identical Verification Submission.
