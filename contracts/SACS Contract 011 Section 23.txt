SACS Contract 011 — Realization Certification, Supervisory Provenance, and Audit
Section 23 — Supervisory Cycle Commitment
Purpose

Section 23 establishes the second and final constitutional commitment boundary within Contract 011.

It determines whether one Constructed Supervisory Cycle Record may become the single authoritative historical representation of the completed supervisory cycle.

This section answers exactly one constitutional question:

May this Constructed Supervisory Cycle Record become the canonical supervisory history for this cycle?

It does not define the contents of the committed artifact.

It authorizes its existence.

Architectural Identity

Supervisory Cycle Commitment deterministically converts one valid Constructed Supervisory Cycle Record into one immutable Committed Supervisory Cycle Record without modifying, reconstructing, extending, or re-adjudicating any upstream constitutional artifact.

Its defining identity is:

Commitment establishes canonical authority; it does not construct constitutional history.

Constitutional Responsibility

Section 23 SHALL:

consume exactly one Constructed Supervisory Cycle Record;
verify every constitutional commitment precondition;
verify Stage Two completion;
verify commitment exclusivity;
verify commitment identity;
perform exactly one atomic commitment;
produce exactly one Supervisory Cycle Commitment Record;
authorize progression to the Committed Supervisory Cycle Record.

Successful completion authorizes Section 24.

Commitment Authority

Section 23 SHALL consume only:

the Constructed Supervisory Cycle Record;
the Committed Realization Record;
the successful Lineage Completeness Evaluation Result;
the successful Lineage Integrity Evaluation Result;
the applicable commitment rules;
applicable contract, schema, serialization, and hashing versions.

It SHALL determine only whether commitment is constitutionally permitted.

It SHALL NOT:

reconstruct the supervisory lineage;
modify the constructed record;
repeat realization certification;
repeat artifact resolution;
repeat relationship resolution;
repeat integrity evaluation;
reinterpret supervisory authority;
perform external verification.
Commitment Preconditions

Commitment SHALL NOT begin unless all of the following are true:

exactly one Constructed Supervisory Cycle Record exists;
the Constructed Supervisory Cycle Record has status CONSTRUCTED;
exactly one Committed Realization Record exists;
Lineage Completeness equals COMPLETE;
Lineage Integrity equals INTEGRITY_VALID;
all required identities are unique;
all commitment identities are internally consistent;
no committed Stage Two failure artifact already exists for the cycle;
no Committed Supervisory Cycle Record already exists except through an authorized idempotent commitment attempt;
the commitment rules are constitutionally fixed.

Failure of any precondition SHALL prevent commitment.

Commitment Verification

Before commitment, Section 23 SHALL verify:

constructed-record identity;
cycle identity;
realization identity;
commitment identity;
schema identity;
serialization identity;
hash identity;
contract-version identity;
exclusivity status.

Commitment SHALL fail if any identity differs from the constitutionally required identity.

Commitment Exclusivity

Exactly one terminal Stage Two authority SHALL exist.

For one supervisory cycle:

Committed Supervisory Cycle Record
XOR
Committed Supervisory Provenance Failure

Both SHALL never exist simultaneously.

Neither SHALL ever be absent after Stage Two terminates.

Atomic Commitment

Commitment SHALL be indivisible.

Either:

Commit Entire Record

or

Commit Nothing

Partial commitment SHALL NOT occur.

No component of the supervisory cycle record may become canonical independently of the whole.

Idempotent Commitment

Repeated commitment requests using an identical Constructed Supervisory Cycle Record SHALL produce the identical canonical commitment outcome.

Repeated requests SHALL NOT create:

multiple committed records;
multiple commitment records;
duplicate supervisory histories;
alternate canonical identities.
Commitment Record

Successful commitment SHALL produce exactly one:

Supervisory Cycle Commitment Record

The commitment record SHALL preserve:

commitment identifier;
supervisory cycle identifier;
constructed record identifier;
committed record identifier;
commitment timestamp;
commitment status;
serialization version;
hashing algorithm;
commitment hash;
applicable contract versions;
applicable schema versions.

The Supervisory Cycle Commitment Record certifies the commitment event.

It is not itself the supervisory history artifact.

Commitment Status

Exactly one terminal commitment status SHALL exist:

COMMITTED

COMMITMENT_FAILED

No intermediate commitment state SHALL be canonical.

Failure Conditions

Representative failures include:

CONSTRUCTED_RECORD_NOT_FOUND

CONSTRUCTED_RECORD_IDENTITY_CONFLICT

REALIZATION_RECORD_MISSING

COMPLETENESS_NOT_COMPLETE

INTEGRITY_NOT_VALID

EXCLUSIVE_RECORD_ALREADY_EXISTS

EXCLUSIVE_FAILURE_ALREADY_EXISTS

COMMITMENT_RULE_MISSING

COMMITMENT_IDENTITY_CONFLICT

ATOMIC_COMMITMENT_FAILED

IDEMPOTENCY_VIOLATION

SERIALIZATION_CONFLICT

HASH_VERIFICATION_FAILED

Failures SHALL identify the precise constitutional reason commitment could not occur.

Failure Consequences

If commitment fails:

no Committed Supervisory Cycle Record SHALL exist;
Section 24 SHALL NOT execute;
Stage Two SHALL terminate through Section 25.

Commitment failure SHALL NOT authorize:

partial commitment;
alternate commitment;
silent retry;
record modification;
record reconstruction;
commitment repair.
Prohibitions

Section 23 SHALL NOT:

modify the Constructed Supervisory Cycle Record;
create additional supervisory history;
reinterpret realization;
reopen execution adjudication;
reopen supervisory continuity adjudication;
broaden authority;
perform replay;
perform audit;
perform external verification.
Determinism

For identical:

Constructed Supervisory Cycle Record;
commitment rules;
contract versions;
schema versions;
serialization rules;
hashing rules;

Supervisory Cycle Commitment SHALL produce an identical commitment result.

No implementation-specific behavior may influence commitment.

Immutability

Once committed:

the Committed Supervisory Cycle Record SHALL be immutable;
the Supervisory Cycle Commitment Record SHALL be immutable;
neither artifact may be modified in place.

Subsequent corrections SHALL occur only through a new constitutional supervisory cycle.
