SACS Contract 011 — Realization Certification, Supervisory Provenance, and Audit
Section 27 — Audit Authority and Audit Representation
Purpose

Section 27 establishes the constitutional authority governing audit of completed supervisory cycles.

Audit evaluates committed constitutional artifacts to determine whether a completed supervisory cycle conforms to constitutional requirements, governance obligations, regulatory policies, contractual commitments, and implementation conformance criteria.

Audit SHALL evaluate constitutional history exactly as committed.

Audit SHALL never create, modify, reinterpret, or replace constitutional authority.

Constitutional Identity

Audit is the constitutional process by which completed supervisory cycles are evaluated using committed constitutional artifacts to determine conformance without altering constitutional history.

Its defining constitutional identity is:

Audit SHALL evaluate constitutional history without becoming a source of constitutional authority.

Audit is evaluative.

Audit is never constitutive.

Constitutional Responsibility

Section 27 SHALL:

establish constitutional audit authority;
define audit inputs;
define audit outputs;
establish audit representation;
define conformance evaluation;
preserve constitutional independence.

Section 27 SHALL NOT:

modify committed artifacts;
authorize supervisory execution;
reconstruct history;
reinterpret supervisory intent;
repair constitutional failures;
replace replay verification.
Canonical Inputs

Audit SHALL consume exactly one completed Stage Two outcome:

one Committed Supervisory Cycle Record; or
one Committed Supervisory Provenance Failure.

Audit MAY additionally consume constitutional evidence produced by replay, including:

Replay Verification Result;
Deterministic Replay diagnostics.

Replay evidence SHALL remain advisory.

Replay SHALL never replace constitutional audit.

Audit SHALL also consume every committed constitutional artifact necessary to evaluate supervisory conformance, including:

Committed Operational Profile;
Committed Reference State;
Committed Control Command;
Committed Translation Transaction;
Committed Execution Disposition;
Committed Supervisory Continuity Record;
Committed Realization Record.
Audit Authority

Audit SHALL determine whether constitutional obligations were satisfied.

Audit SHALL NOT determine new supervisory intent.

Audit SHALL NOT authorize execution.

Audit SHALL NOT alter constitutional history.

Audit conclusions SHALL describe constitutional conformance only.

Audit Evaluation

Audit SHALL evaluate:

constitutional identity;
supervisory authority;
commitment integrity;
artifact lineage;
provenance completeness;
provenance integrity;
commitment ordering;
replay equivalence (where available);
contract conformance;
governance conformance;
implementation conformance.

Audit MAY evaluate additional implementation evidence provided it does not alter constitutional meaning.

Recommendations

Recommendations SHALL possess no constitutional authority unless separately adopted through constitutional governance mechanisms.

Supporting Evidence

Immutable references to:

evaluated committed artifacts;
replay verification results;
applicable governance artifacts;
regulatory evidence;
implementation evidence.
Audit Status

Exactly one terminal audit status SHALL be assigned:

CONFORMANT

NON_CONFORMANT

INCONCLUSIVE

These statuses describe evaluation results only.

They SHALL NOT modify constitutional authority.

Audit Independence

Audit SHALL remain constitutionally independent from:

supervisory execution;
replay;
governance decisions;
implementation architecture.

Audit conclusions SHALL not depend upon implementation-specific behavior outside the committed constitutional record.

Preservation Requirements

Audit SHALL preserve:

constitutional identity;
supervisory identity;
artifact identity;
provenance relationships;
evaluation evidence;
finding traceability;
historical ordering.

Audit SHALL never obscure or replace constitutional history.

Determinism

Identical committed constitutional artifacts SHALL always produce identical audit conclusions when evaluated under identical audit rules.

Audit SHALL remain deterministic regardless of:

implementation platform;
storage architecture;
deployment topology;
execution environment.
Relationship to Governance

Audit findings MAY inform future governance actions.

Audit SHALL NOT itself enact governance.

Governance remains exclusively responsible for constitutional policy modification.

Audit therefore provides evidence for governance rather than governance itself.

Prohibitions

Section 27 SHALL NOT:

execute supervisory control;
authorize runtime behavior;
modify committed artifacts;
reconstruct constitutional history;
reinterpret supervisory authority;
repair failures;
replace governance;
create new constitutional records.

Section 28
Fundamental Constitutional Invariant

Sections 24 and 25 establish the authoritative supervisory outcome.

Section 26 verifies that the committed outcome is constitutionally reproducible.

Section 27 evaluates that committed outcome for constitutional, governance, regulatory, and implementation conformance.

Section 28 concludes the contract by expressing the single constitutional property preserved by every preceding section.

Fundamental Constitutional Principle

Audit SHALL evaluate completed supervisory cycles exclusively through committed constitutional artifacts, preserving constitutional identity, supervisory authority, provenance, commitment integrity, and historical truth without creating, modifying, replacing, or reinterpreting constitutional authority.
