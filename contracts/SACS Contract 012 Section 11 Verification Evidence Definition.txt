SACS Contract 012 — External Verification Profile and Evidence Interface
Section 11 — Verification Evidence Definition
Purpose

Section 11 defines the canonical constitutional vocabulary of verification evidence recognized by Contract 012.

A Verification Evidence Definition establishes the exact meaning, identity, authority, admissibility rules, integrity requirements, provenance requirements, lifecycle, and relationships of one class of evidence that may participate in external verification.

Verification Evidence Definitions are reusable constitutional artifacts.

They enable Verification Profiles, Verification Requirement Sets, and Verification Evidence Packages to reference evidence classes through stable, versioned constitutional definitions rather than redefining evidence semantics for every verification operation.

This section ensures that every reference to evidence possesses an explicit constitutional meaning before evidence is resolved or assembled.

No Verification Evidence Package may be constructed without first resolving every referenced evidence class through an adopted Verification Evidence Definition.

Constitutional Question

Section 11 answers:

What constitutes constitutional verification evidence?

A Verification Evidence Definition SHALL establish:

what the evidence represents;
which evidence class it belongs to;
which verification subjects it may support;
which claim types it may support;
which verification methods may consume it;
how its authority is established;
how its integrity is preserved;
how its provenance is represented;
how its admissibility is determined;
how its lifecycle is governed.

An evidence name alone SHALL NOT establish constitutional meaning.

Constitutional Identity

The defining constitutional identity of a Verification Evidence Definition is:

A Verification Evidence Definition is one reusable, immutable, versioned constitutional artifact that defines a recognized class of verification evidence without identifying any specific evidence instance, satisfying any verification requirement, or establishing the truth of any verification claim.

A Verification Evidence Definition is not:

a Verification Evidence Instance;
a Verification Claim;
a Verification Requirement;
a Verification Method;
a Verification Submission;
an External Verification Result.

Formally:

Verification Evidence Definition
≠
Verification Evidence Instance

and

Verification Evidence
≠
Verification Claim
Evidence Authority

Contract 012 owns the constitutional representation, registration, validation, resolution, and supersession of Verification Evidence Definitions.

Contract 012 MAY:

define evidence-definition schemas;
register approved evidence definitions;
resolve evidence definitions by identity and version;
validate definition integrity;
bind evidence definitions into Verification Profiles;
reference evidence definitions within Verification Requirement Sets;
preserve definition lineage;
preserve supersession relationships.

Contract 012 SHALL NOT:

construct evidence instances;
determine that evidence satisfies a claim;
infer evidence classes from implementation behavior;
silently substitute one evidence class for another;
weaken admissibility requirements.
Evidence Definition and Evidence Instance

The distinction SHALL remain:

Verification Evidence Definition
≠
Verification Evidence Instance

A Verification Evidence Definition specifies the reusable constitutional meaning of one evidence class.

A Verification Evidence Instance represents one operation-specific realization of that class.

The definition is reusable.

The instance is operation-specific.

COMMITTED_ARTIFACT

EXTERNAL_REFERENCE

FAILURE_RECORD
LINEAGE_RECORD

Each evidence class SHALL be defined through one or more Verification Evidence Definitions.

An evidence-class label SHALL NOT substitute for a versioned Verification Evidence Definition.

Evidence Purpose

Every Verification Evidence Definition SHALL identify the verification purposes it may support.

Examples include:

claim substantiation;
lineage demonstration;
integrity validation;
provenance verification;
deterministic replay;
implementation conformance;
authority preservation;
temporal verification;
cryptographic verification.

An evidence definition SHALL NOT be represented as supporting purposes outside its declared capability.

Supported Claim Types

Every Verification Evidence Definition SHALL define the claim types it may support.

Support for a claim type SHALL NOT imply that the evidence alone establishes the truth of that claim.

Evidence contributes to verification.

It does not constitute verification.

Supported Subject Types

Each Verification Evidence Definition SHALL identify the verification subjects to which it applies.

A definition applicable to one subject type SHALL NOT automatically apply to another.

Required Source Classes

Every Verification Evidence Definition SHALL define the classes of sources from which valid evidence instances may originate.

Examples MAY include:

committed constitutional artifacts;
registry authorities;
implementation repositories;
cryptographic authorities;
runtime instrumentation;
external verification systems.

An evidence instance originating from an unauthorized source SHALL NOT satisfy the definition.

Evidence Admissibility

Every Verification Evidence Definition SHALL establish the admissibility conditions required for evidence instances belonging to that definition.

Admissibility SHALL remain distinct from sufficiency.

Formally:

Admissible Evidence
≠
Sufficient Evidence
Evidence Integrity

Every Verification Evidence Definition SHALL define the integrity conditions required before an evidence instance may participate in verification.

Integrity requirements MAY include:

cryptographic hashes;
digital signatures;
commitment validation;
schema validation;
chain-of-custody validation;
immutable-reference validation.

Integrity establishes authenticity.

It does not establish correctness.

Evidence Provenance

Every Verification Evidence Definition SHALL define the provenance information required of every evidence instance.

Provenance SHALL preserve:

origin;
producing authority;
producing contract;
producing artifact;
creation time;
version;
lineage relationships.

Evidence SHALL NOT lose provenance through later packaging.

Evidence Relationships

A Verification Evidence Definition MAY define constitutional relationships to other evidence definitions.

Relationship types MAY include:

REQUIRES
SUPPLEMENTS
DERIVED_FROM
CORROBORATES
SUPERSEDES
CONFLICTS_WITH

Relationships SHALL remain explicit.

Evidence Lifecycle

Every Verification Evidence Definition SHALL define the lifecycle governing evidence instances.

The lifecycle MAY identify:

creation;
commitment;
eligibility;
admissibility;
supersession;
expiration;
archival;
replay eligibility.

Lifecycle SHALL remain distinct from verification outcome.

Evidence Supersession

Supersession SHALL preserve constitutional lineage.

A superseded evidence definition SHALL remain historically resolvable.

Supersession SHALL NOT invalidate previously committed verification submissions.
