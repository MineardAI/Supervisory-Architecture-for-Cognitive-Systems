## SACS Contract 011 — Realization Certification, Supervisory Provenance, and Audit

# Section 20 — Lineage Completeness Evaluation

## Purpose

Section 20 establishes the constitutional authority responsible for determining whether the Constructed Supervisory Lineage produced under Section 19 contains every artifact, relationship, dependency, and authority path required to represent one complete supervisory cycle.

This section answers exactly one constitutional question:

> **Does the constructed supervisory lineage contain everything constitutionally required for this supervisory cycle?**

Lineage Completeness Evaluation does not determine whether the included information is correct, internally consistent, authentic, or trustworthy.

Those questions belong to Lineage Integrity Evaluation.

Completeness asks only whether the required constitutional structure is present.

---

# Architectural Identity

Lineage Completeness Evaluation deterministically verifies the presence of all required lineage elements without reconstructing, substituting, inferring, or validating their content.

Its defining identity is:

> **Completeness determines whether the required constitutional lineage is present; it does not determine whether that lineage is valid.**

---

# Constitutional Responsibility

Lineage Completeness Evaluation SHALL:

* evaluate required artifact membership within the Constructed Supervisory Lineage;
* evaluate required relationship membership as resolved under Section 18;
* evaluate required derivation paths;
* evaluate required authority-preservation paths;
* evaluate required cycle identity bindings;
* evaluate required terminal lineage;
* identify every missing or unresolved lineage element;
* produce exactly one canonical completeness result.

Successful completion authorizes progression to Lineage Integrity Evaluation.

---

# Completeness Authority

Lineage Completeness Evaluation SHALL consume only:

* the Canonical Artifact Resolution Set (Section 17);
* the Resolved Constitutional Relationship Set (Section 18);
* the Constructed Supervisory Lineage (Section 19);
* the applicable committed lineage requirements;
* the applicable schema and contract-version bindings.

It SHALL determine only whether all required lineage elements are present.

It SHALL NOT determine:

* whether an artifact’s contents are valid;
* whether a relationship is semantically correct;
* whether evidence is authentic;
* whether a commitment is cryptographically valid;
* whether timestamps are truthful;
* whether the supervisory outcome was correct;
* whether external verification succeeds.

---

# Required Lineage Domain

The minimum directly resolved Contract 011 supervisory lineage SHALL contain exactly one canonical instance of each required constitutional role as represented in the Constructed Supervisory Lineage:

```text
Committed Operational Profile

Committed Reference State

Committed Control Error

Committed Control Command

Committed Translation Transaction

Committed Execution Disposition

Committed Supervisory Continuity Record

Committed Realization Record
```

These artifacts form the directly resolved Contract 006–011 supervisory spine as constructed under Section 19.

Earlier constitutional artifacts, including Observation, Committed Operational State Vector, Committed Governance Snapshot, and Committed Objective Set, MAY remain reachable through preserved upstream references represented as external lineage references.

Unless explicitly required by the committed lineage requirements, those earlier artifacts SHALL NOT be independently reconstructed or added as direct lineage members.

---

# Required Relationship Domain

The Constructed Supervisory Lineage SHALL contain every required constitutional relationship among the resolved artifacts as defined by the Resolved Constitutional Relationship Set.

At minimum, the following relationships SHALL be represented where established by the owning contracts:

```text
Committed Operational Profile
        ↓ configures / binds
Committed Reference State

Committed Reference State
        ↓ participates in derivation of
Committed Control Error

Committed Control Error
        ↓ participates in derivation of
Committed Control Command

Committed Control Command
        ↓ translated into
Committed Translation Transaction

Committed Translation Transaction
        ↓ adjudicated through
Committed Execution Disposition

Committed Execution Disposition
        ↓ referenced by
Committed Supervisory Continuity Record

Committed Execution Disposition
        +
Committed Supervisory Continuity Record
        ↓ certified into
Committed Realization Record
```

The exact relationship type, direction, and authority SHALL be those resolved under Section 18 and preserved in the Constructed Supervisory Lineage.

Completeness Evaluation SHALL NOT replace those relationship types with simplified or implementation-defined edges.

---

# Required Completeness Dimensions

Lineage Completeness Evaluation SHALL evaluate the following dimensions independently.

## Artifact Completeness

Every required constitutional role SHALL resolve to exactly one committed artifact present as a direct lineage member in the Constructed Supervisory Lineage.

Formally:

[
\forall r \in R(c),\ |Resolve(r)| = 1
]

Where:

* (c) is the supervisory cycle;
* (R(c)) is the set of required constitutional roles.

---

## Relationship Completeness

Every relationship required by the applicable contract versions SHALL be present in the Constructed Supervisory Lineage and SHALL correspond to a resolved relationship identity from Section 18.

Formally:

[
E_{required}(c) \subseteq E_{present}(c)
]

No required edge may be absent.

---

## Path Completeness

Every required artifact SHALL be connected to the supervisory lineage through at least one constitutionally valid path composed only of resolved relationships.

No required artifact may remain isolated within the constructed graph.

---

## Authority-Path Completeness

Every authority-bearing artifact SHALL preserve a traceable path, through resolved authority-preservation relationships, to the authority from which it was derived or constrained.

Authority-path completeness verifies only the presence of such paths.

It does not validate the authority itself.

---

## Cycle-Binding Completeness

Every directly resolved artifact SHALL be bound to the same supervisory cycle or to an explicitly authorized cross-cycle relationship as represented in the Constructed Supervisory Lineage.

Unexplained cross-cycle membership SHALL fail completeness.

---

## Commitment-Binding Completeness

Every canonical artifact SHALL preserve its required commitment identity and commitment reference as represented in the Constructed Supervisory Lineage.

A constructed or proposed artifact SHALL NOT satisfy a committed-artifact requirement.

---

## Terminal-Lineage Completeness

The lineage SHALL terminate in exactly one:

* Committed Realization Record; or
* explicit Stage One failure artifact.

For a successful Stage Two evaluation, the terminal artifact SHALL be the Committed Realization Record represented as the terminal vertex in the Constructed Supervisory Lineage.

A provisional or constructed realization record SHALL NOT satisfy terminal-lineage completeness.

---

# Completeness Requirement Set

Every evaluation SHALL be governed by one immutable Completeness Requirement Set.

The requirement set SHALL identify:

* supervisory cycle identity;
* applicable contract versions;
* required artifact roles;
* required relationship types;
* required path constraints;
* required commitment bindings;
* permitted external references;
* permitted cross-cycle references;
* optional lineage elements;
* completeness failure codes;
* deterministic evaluation order.

The Completeness Requirement Set SHALL be committed or otherwise constitutionally fixed before evaluation begins.

It SHALL NOT be modified during evaluation.

---

# Optional Lineage Elements

A supervisory lineage MAY contain optional committed references represented either as direct lineage members or external lineage references where permitted by the applicable contracts.

Optional lineage elements SHALL:

* preserve their exact identity;
* be explicitly classified as optional;
* remain distinguishable from required lineage;
* never compensate for a missing required element;
* never broaden the constitutional scope of the lineage.

The presence of optional lineage SHALL NOT convert an incomplete required lineage into a complete one.

---

# Absence Semantics

Every absent lineage element SHALL be classified explicitly.

Representative absence classes include:

```text
NOT_REQUIRED

OPTIONALLY_ABSENT

REQUIRED_BUT_UNRESOLVED

REQUIRED_RELATIONSHIP_MISSING

REQUIRED_PATH_MISSING

COMMITMENT_BINDING_MISSING

CYCLE_BINDING_MISSING

EXTERNAL_REFERENCE_UNAVAILABLE
```

Null, omission, unavailable, unsupported, and unresolved SHALL NOT be treated as equivalent states.

---

# Evaluation Ordering

Lineage Completeness Evaluation SHALL execute in deterministic order.

The minimum evaluation sequence SHALL be:

```text
1. Requirement-set binding
2. Cycle-identity binding
3. Required artifact membership (from Constructed Supervisory Lineage)
4. Required commitment binding
5. Required relationship membership (from resolved relationships)
6. Required path evaluation
7. Authority-path evaluation
8. Terminal-lineage evaluation
9. Optional-element classification
10. Completeness result construction
```

An implementation MAY optimize execution internally, but the observable result SHALL be equivalent to this canonical ordering.

---

# Completeness Result

Successful evaluation SHALL produce exactly one internal constitutional artifact:

> **Lineage Completeness Evaluation Result**

The result SHALL contain:

* supervisory cycle identity;
* Constructed Supervisory Lineage identity;
* Canonical Artifact Resolution Set identity;
* Resolved Constitutional Relationship Set identity;
* Completeness Requirement Set identity;
* applicable contract versions;
* required artifact results;
* required relationship results;
* required path results;
* authority-path results;
* cycle-binding results;
* commitment-binding results;
* optional-element classifications;
* terminal-lineage result;
* completeness status;
* failure codes, if any;
* deterministic evaluation timestamp;
* result hash.

The result is an internal constitutional working artifact.

It SHALL NOT constitute a committed supervisory history artifact.

---

# Completeness Status

The Lineage Completeness Evaluation Result SHALL produce exactly one terminal status:

```text
COMPLETE

INCOMPLETE
```

No intermediate, probable, assumed, substantially complete, or best-effort status SHALL be canonical.

If any required element is absent or unresolved:

```text
Completeness = INCOMPLETE
```

If every required element is present:

```text
Completeness = COMPLETE
```

---

# Failure Conditions

Representative completeness failures include:

```text
REQUIRED_ARTIFACT_MISSING

REQUIRED_RELATIONSHIP_MISSING

REQUIRED_LINEAGE_PATH_MISSING

AUTHORITY_PATH_MISSING

CYCLE_BINDING_INCOMPLETE

COMMITMENT_BINDING_INCOMPLETE

TERMINAL_REALIZATION_MISSING

MULTIPLE_TERMINAL_REALIZATIONS

UNRESOLVED_EXTERNAL_REFERENCE

COMPLETENESS_REQUIREMENT_SET_MISSING

COMPLETENESS_REQUIREMENT_SET_CONFLICT

OPTIONAL_ARTIFACT_USED_AS_SUBSTITUTE

PROPOSED_ARTIFACT_USED_AS_COMMITTED

CROSS_CYCLE_LINEAGE_UNAUTHORIZED
```

Failure codes SHALL identify the exact missing or unresolved condition.

Generic completeness failure SHALL NOT replace a more specific available failure code.

---

# Failure Consequences

If the lineage is incomplete:

* Lineage Integrity Evaluation SHALL NOT begin;
* the Constructed Supervisory Cycle Record SHALL NOT be created;
* Supervisory Cycle Commitment SHALL NOT begin;
* no Committed Supervisory Cycle Record SHALL exist;
* Stage Two SHALL progress toward Committed Supervisory Provenance Failure.

Completeness failure SHALL NOT authorize:

* artifact reconstruction;
* relationship inference;
* substitution;
* default lineage insertion;
* use of a newer artifact;
* use of a similar artifact;
* use of an uncommitted artifact;
* partial cycle commitment.

---

# Prohibitions

Lineage Completeness Evaluation SHALL NOT:

* infer missing artifacts;
* reconstruct absent relationships;
* repair broken lineage;
* validate artifact contents;
* evaluate cryptographic integrity;
* reinterpret upstream authority;
* merge supervisory cycles;
* substitute optional lineage for required lineage;
* treat reachability as proof of integrity;
* commit the supervisory cycle record;
* perform external verification.

---

# Determinism

For identical:

* Constructed Supervisory Lineage;
* Canonical Artifact Resolution Set;
* Resolved Constitutional Relationship Set;
* Completeness Requirement Set;
* contract versions;
* schema versions;

Lineage Completeness Evaluation SHALL produce an identical result.

Formally:

[
Complete(L, Q) = Complete(L, Q)
]

for identical lineage (L) and requirement set (Q).

No ambient implementation state may affect the result.

---

# Immutability

Once constructed, the Lineage Completeness Evaluation Result SHALL be immutable.

A later correction SHALL produce a new result with:

* a new result identity;
* explicit supersession lineage;
* preserved reference to the earlier result;
* no in-place modification.

A changed result does not retroactively alter the lineage that was evaluated.

---

# Relationship to Neighboring Sections

The constitutional progression SHALL be:

```text
Section 19
Lineage Graph Construction
        │
        ▼
Section 20
Lineage Completeness Evaluation
        │
        ▼
Section 21
Lineage Integrity Evaluation
```

These sections answer distinct constitutional questions:

* **Section 19** — What lineage was constructed?
* **Section 20** — Is every required lineage element present?
* **Section 21** — Are the present lineage elements internally valid and constitutionally coherent?

Completeness SHALL operate strictly on the constructed lineage and resolved relationships.

Completeness SHALL precede integrity.

An incomplete lineage cannot receive a successful integrity determination.

---

# Mathematical Interpretation

Let:

* (A_r) be the required artifact set;
* (A_p) be the present artifact set derived from the Constructed Supervisory Lineage;
* (E_r) be the required relationship set;
* (E_p) be the present relationship set derived from the Resolved Constitutional Relationship Set;
* (P_r) be the required path set;
* (P_p) be the present path set within the constructed lineage.

The lineage is complete exactly when:

[
A_r \subseteq A_p
]

and:

[
E_r \subseteq E_p
]

and:

[
P_r \subseteq P_p
]

and every required artifact resolves exactly once:

[
\forall r \in R(c),\ |Resolve(r)| = 1
]

Therefore:

[
Complete(L) =
\begin{cases}
true, & \text{if all required membership, relationship, path, cycle, and commitment conditions hold} \
false, & \text{otherwise}
\end{cases}
]

Completeness does not imply integrity:

[
Complete(L) \nRightarrow IntegrityValid(L)
]

---

# Fundamental Completeness Principle

The defining constitutional principle of this section is:

> **Lineage Completeness Evaluation SHALL deterministically determine whether every required committed artifact, resolved constitutional relationship, derivation path, authority path, cycle binding, commitment binding, and terminal realization element is present within one constructed supervisory lineage, without inferring, reconstructing, substituting, validating, or reinterpreting any missing or included constitutional information.**
