SACS Contract 011 — Realization Certification, Supervisory Provenance, and Audit
Section 10 — Realization Outcome Classification
Purpose

Section 10 establishes the constitutional authority responsible for classifying the historical realization represented by the Constructed Realization Timeline.

Realization Outcome Classification determines the constitutional realization state supported by the admissible committed supervisory evidence.

This section answers exactly one constitutional question:

What authoritative realization outcome is established by the committed historical timeline?

It does not certify realization, construct historical records, assemble provenance, or evaluate external correctness.

Constitutional Responsibility

Realization Outcome Classification SHALL:

evaluate the Constructed Realization Timeline;
determine the constitutional realization outcome;
preserve every committed authority established by preceding contracts;
produce one canonical realization outcome classification.

Successful completion authorizes progression to Realization Certification Determination.

Outcome Classification Authority

Outcome classification SHALL be derived exclusively from:

the Constructed Realization Timeline;
admitted committed constitutional evidence;
the realization-certification context.

Classification SHALL NOT depend upon:

implementation-specific heuristics;
runtime assumptions;
external observations;
inferred supervisory behavior;
reconstructed historical events.
Canonical Outcome Classes

Every realization SHALL resolve to exactly one canonical realization outcome.

Representative outcome classes include:

REALIZED

PARTIALLY_REALIZED

EXECUTION_REJECTED

EXECUTION_DEFERRED

EXECUTION_RESTRICTED

EXECUTION_ABORTED

EXECUTION_FAULTED

ROLLED_BACK

COMPENSATED

REALIZATION_INDETERMINATE

Additional constitutional outcome classes MAY be introduced only through future revisions of this contract.

Implementations SHALL NOT invent implementation-specific outcome classes.

Classification Rules

Outcome Classification SHALL preserve the determinations established by earlier contracts.

Accordingly:

execution disposition SHALL remain the authority of Contract 009;
supervisory continuity SHALL remain the authority of Contract 010;
temporal ordering SHALL remain the authority of Section 9.

This section SHALL determine only the constitutional realization outcome implied by those committed determinations.

Outcome Preservation

Successful classification SHALL preserve:

supervisory cycle identity;
realization identity;
execution disposition references;
supervisory continuity references;
temporal ordering;
uncertainty representation;
constitutional authority.

Classification SHALL organize constitutional history.

It SHALL NOT modify constitutional history.

Outcome Constraints

Realization Outcome Classification SHALL NOT:

certify realization history;
alter execution disposition;
alter supervisory continuity;
reinterpret controller behavior;
evaluate operational correctness;
determine external truth;
infer missing historical evidence.

The realization outcome SHALL be determined exclusively from the committed constitutional history established by preceding sections.

Classification Failure

Representative failures include:

REALIZATION_OUTCOME_CONFLICT

REALIZATION_CLASSIFICATION_AMBIGUOUS

MULTIPLE_VALID_OUTCOMES

NO_CONSTITUTIONAL_OUTCOME

OUTCOME_AUTHORITY_CONFLICT

OUTCOME_DETERMINATION_FAULT

Upon failure:

Realization Certification Determination SHALL NOT begin;
no Realization Record SHALL be constructed;
provenance construction SHALL NOT begin.

Failure SHALL be represented explicitly.

Constitutional Output

Successful completion SHALL produce exactly one:

Realization Outcome Classification

The Realization Outcome Classification SHALL contain:

realization-certification context;
canonical realization outcome;
supporting historical references;
execution disposition reference;
supervisory continuity reference;
determination timestamp.

This artifact is an internal constitutional working artifact.

It SHALL NOT constitute committed constitutional history.

Architectural Constraints

Realization Outcome Classification is a classification authority.

It SHALL NOT:

certify realization;
construct the Realization Record;
commit historical artifacts;
assemble supervisory provenance;
perform replay;
perform audit.

Its sole constitutional responsibility is determining the canonical realization outcome supported by the committed historical evidence.

Relationship to Neighboring Sections

The constitutional progression SHALL be:

Section 9
Realization Timeline Construction
        │
        ▼
Section 10
Realization Outcome Classification
        │
        ▼
Section 11

The distinction between these sections is fundamental:

Section 9 constructs the historical sequence.
Section 10 classifies the realization represented by that sequence.
Section 11 determines whether that classified realization is constitutionally eligible to become authoritative supervisory history.

Classification is therefore not certification.

Fundamental Classification Principle

The defining principle of this section is:

Realization Outcome Classification SHALL determine exactly one canonical realization outcome from the committed constitutional history established by preceding sections. It SHALL preserve every prior constitutional determination without modification, reinterpretation, or expansion of supervisory authority.
