SACS Contract 011 — Realization Certification, Supervisory Provenance, and Audit
Section 11 — Realization Certification Determination
Purpose

Section 11 establishes the constitutional authority responsible for determining whether the classified supervisory realization is eligible to become authoritative constitutional history.

Realization Certification Determination is the final constitutional adjudication performed before the construction of the Realization Record.

This section answers exactly one constitutional question:

Is the classified supervisory realization constitutionally certifiable?

It does not construct the Realization Record, commit historical artifacts, assemble provenance, or perform external verification.

Constitutional Responsibility

Realization Certification Determination SHALL:

evaluate the Realization Outcome Classification;
determine whether constitutional certification requirements have been satisfied;
preserve every constitutional authority established by preceding sections;
produce one canonical certification determination.

Successful completion authorizes progression to Constructed Realization Record.

Certification Authority

Certification SHALL determine only whether the committed constitutional evidence establishes one authoritative supervisory realization.

Certification SHALL NOT determine:

objective external truth;
implementation correctness;
runtime correctness;
physical system correctness;
future supervisory behavior.

Certification establishes constitutional historical authority, not external factual certainty.

Certification Criteria

A realization SHALL be certifiable only when all of the following are true:

required committed evidence was successfully received;
constitutional identity and binding were successfully validated;
evidence was constitutionally admissible;
a deterministic realization timeline was successfully constructed;
exactly one canonical realization outcome was successfully classified;
no unresolved constitutional conflicts remain.

Failure of any mandatory criterion SHALL result in certification failure.

Certification Determination

Exactly one certification determination SHALL be produced.

The determination SHALL be one of:

CERTIFIABLE

NOT_CERTIFIABLE

Intermediate certification states SHALL NOT exist.

Partial certification SHALL NOT exist.

Multiple simultaneous certification determinations SHALL NOT exist.

Certification Preservation

Certification SHALL preserve:

realization identity;
supervisory cycle identity;
execution disposition identity;
supervisory continuity identity;
timeline identity;
realization outcome identity;
constitutional authority.

Certification SHALL introduce no new supervisory information.

It certifies the constitutional sufficiency of information already established.

Certification Constraints

Realization Certification Determination SHALL NOT:

modify the realization outcome;
modify committed evidence;
reopen execution disposition;
reopen supervisory continuity;
infer missing historical evidence;
reconstruct historical events;
resolve provenance;
perform replay;
perform audit.

Certification is an adjudicative authority.

It is not a constructive authority.

Certification Failure

Representative constitutional failures include:

REALIZATION_NOT_CERTIFIABLE

CERTIFICATION_REQUIREMENTS_UNSATISFIED

UNRESOLVED_CONSTITUTIONAL_CONFLICT

HISTORICAL_AUTHORITY_INSUFFICIENT

CERTIFICATION_CONTEXT_INVALID

REALIZATION_CERTIFICATION_FAULT

Upon certification failure:

no Realization Record SHALL be constructed;
no historical commitment SHALL occur;
supervisory provenance SHALL NOT begin.

Failure SHALL be represented explicitly.

Constitutional Output

Successful completion SHALL produce exactly one:

Realization Certification Determination

The determination SHALL contain:

realization-certification context;
certification status;
realization outcome reference;
constitutional basis for certification;
supporting determination references;
determination timestamp.

The Realization Certification Determination is an internal constitutional working artifact.

It SHALL NOT constitute committed constitutional history.

Architectural Constraints

Realization Certification Determination is the terminal adjudicative authority of Stage One.

It SHALL NOT:

construct the Realization Record;
commit historical artifacts;
construct Supervisory Cycle Records;
establish lineage;
perform replay;
perform audit.

Its sole constitutional responsibility is determining whether the supervisory realization is eligible to become authoritative constitutional history.

These sections perform three distinct constitutional functions:

Section 10 determines what realization occurred.
Section 11 determines whether that realization may become authoritative constitutional history.
Section 12 constructs the canonical historical representation of that certified realization.

No section SHALL assume the authority of another.

Fundamental Certification Principle

The defining principle of this section is:

Realization Certification Determination SHALL establish whether one classified supervisory realization is constitutionally eligible to become authoritative historical record. Certification SHALL preserve every prior constitutional determination without modifying, extending, or reinterpreting committed supervisory evidence, and SHALL establish constitutional historical authority only—not externally verified truth.
