SACS Contract 011 — Realization Certification, Supervisory Provenance, and Audit
Section 24 — Committed Supervisory Cycle Record
Purpose

Section 24 defines the constitutional identity, authority, structure, and lifecycle of the Committed Supervisory Cycle Record.

The Committed Supervisory Cycle Record is the sole authoritative historical representation of one completed supervisory cycle.

It exists only following successful completion of the Supervisory Cycle Commitment defined in Section 23.

This section defines the committed artifact itself. It does not authorize commitment, reconstruct provenance, repeat realization certification, perform replay, or conduct audit activities.

Constitutional Identity

The Committed Supervisory Cycle Record is the immutable constitutional record representing the complete supervisory history of one supervisory cycle.

It combines, without reinterpretation or modification, the authoritative outputs produced throughout Stage Two into one canonical historical artifact suitable for replay, audit, governance review, regulatory inspection, and constitutional traceability.

Its defining constitutional identity is:

One supervisory cycle SHALL produce exactly one immutable Committed Supervisory Cycle Record—or exactly one Committed Supervisory Provenance Failure—but never both.

Constitutional Responsibility

Section 24 SHALL:

define the authoritative supervisory history artifact;
establish its constitutional identity;
define its required contents;
define its relationship to upstream committed artifacts;
establish immutability requirements;
establish provenance preservation requirements;
establish uniqueness requirements;
define its relationship to replay and audit.

Section 24 SHALL NOT:

authorize commitment;
repeat realization certification;
reconstruct lineage;
modify historical evidence;
reinterpret supervisory authority;
execute replay;
perform audit;
authorize execution.
Canonical Inputs

The Committed Supervisory Cycle Record SHALL be derived exclusively from:

one successful Supervisory Cycle Commitment;
one Constructed Supervisory Cycle Record;
one Committed Realization Record;
one successful Lineage Completeness Evaluation Result;
one successful Lineage Integrity Evaluation Result;
one Supervisory Cycle Commitment Record.

No additional information may be introduced during commitment.

Constitutional Authority

The Committed Supervisory Cycle Record SHALL become the sole constitutional authority representing the completed supervisory cycle.

Once committed, it SHALL supersede the Constructed Supervisory Cycle Record as the authoritative historical artifact.

The Constructed Supervisory Cycle Record SHALL remain historical implementation evidence but SHALL possess no independent constitutional authority after commitment.

Preservation Requirements

The Committed Supervisory Cycle Record SHALL preserve:

constitutional identity;
supervisory intent;
authority relationships;
artifact identity;
commitment ordering;
temporal ordering;
provenance relationships;
execution lineage.

No information required for constitutional replay or audit may be removed.

Immutability

After commitment:

the Committed Supervisory Cycle Record SHALL never be modified;
historical relationships SHALL never be rewritten;
identifiers SHALL never change;
hashes SHALL never change;
commitment identity SHALL never change.

Any correction SHALL require initiation of a new supervisory cycle.

Uniqueness

For every Supervisory Cycle Identifier:

Exactly one Committed Supervisory Cycle Record SHALL exist.

Formally:

COUNT(Committed Supervisory Cycle Record
WHERE SupervisoryCycleID = X) = 1

Duplicate committed records SHALL constitute a constitutional violation.

Relationship to Replay

The Committed Supervisory Cycle Record SHALL serve as the authoritative historical input to deterministic replay defined in Section 26.

Replay SHALL consume this committed artifact without modification.

Replay SHALL NOT reconstruct constitutional history from lower-level artifacts when a valid Committed Supervisory Cycle Record exists.

Relationship to Audit

The Committed Supervisory Cycle Record SHALL serve as the primary authoritative evidence consumed by audit processes defined in Section 27.

Audit SHALL evaluate the committed record.

Audit SHALL NOT redefine the committed record.

Failure Relationship

If commitment fails:

No Committed Supervisory Cycle Record SHALL exist.

Instead, the supervisory cycle SHALL terminate through the creation of one Committed Supervisory Provenance Failure defined in Section 25.

Both artifacts SHALL never exist simultaneously.

Determinism

Identical constitutional inputs SHALL always produce identical Committed Supervisory Cycle Records.

Implementation architecture, storage platform, serialization implementation, execution environment, or deployment topology SHALL not affect the committed constitutional identity.

Prohibitions

Section 24 SHALL NOT:

reinterpret supervisory intent;
reconstruct historical evidence;
perform validation already completed by earlier sections;
authorize execution;
authorize replay;
perform audit;
broaden constitutional authority;
modify committed artifacts.

Fundamental Constitutional Principle

The Committed Supervisory Cycle Record SHALL constitute the single immutable, authoritative constitutional history of one completed supervisory cycle, preserving supervisory identity, realization, provenance, authority, commitment, and traceability exactly as committed, without modification, reinterpretation, or duplication.
