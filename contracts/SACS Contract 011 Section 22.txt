## SACS Contract 011 — Realization Certification, Supervisory Provenance, and Audit

# Section 22 — Constructed Supervisory Cycle Record

## Purpose

Section 22 establishes the constitutional authority responsible for constructing one proposed whole-cycle record from the complete and integrity-valid supervisory lineage established under Sections 17–21.

This section answers exactly one constitutional question:

> **What exact whole-cycle record is constructed from the complete and integrity-valid supervisory lineage?**

The Constructed Supervisory Cycle Record organizes the authoritative Stage One realization and the validated Stage Two provenance materials into one deterministic precommit representation.

It does not make that representation canonical.

Canonical authority is established only through Supervisory Cycle Commitment under Section 23.

---

# Architectural Identity

Supervisory Cycle Record Construction deterministically assembles one complete and integrity-valid supervisory lineage into one proposed whole-cycle record without modifying, re-adjudicating, supplementing, or recommitting any upstream artifact.

Its defining identity is:

> **The Constructed Supervisory Cycle Record represents the complete constitutional history of one supervisory cycle before final commitment; construction does not make that history canonical.**

---

# Constitutional Responsibility

Section 22 SHALL:

* consume only successful Stage Two resolution, construction, completeness, and integrity artifacts;
* bind the Committed Realization Record to its complete supervisory lineage;
* bind the exact cross-contract artifact membership;
* bind the exact constitutional relationships;
* bind the exact authority and derivation paths;
* bind completeness and integrity results;
* construct the cycle-level audit index;
* construct the cycle-level replay manifest;
* preserve all required external lineage references;
* produce exactly one Constructed Supervisory Cycle Record.

Successful construction authorizes progression to Supervisory Cycle Commitment.

---

# Construction Authority

Supervisory Cycle Record Construction SHALL consume only:

* the Committed Realization Record;
* the Canonical Artifact Resolution Set from Section 17;
* the Resolved Constitutional Relationship Set from Section 18;
* the Constructed Supervisory Lineage from Section 19;
* a `COMPLETE` Lineage Completeness Evaluation Result from Section 20;
* an `INTEGRITY_VALID` Lineage Integrity Evaluation Result from Section 21;
* the applicable Supervisory Cycle Record Definition;
* the applicable contract, schema, registry, serialization, and hashing versions.

It SHALL determine only:

* the canonical precommit structure of the whole-cycle record;
* the exact membership of the record;
* the exact ordering of record components;
* the binding of lineage, audit, replay, and validation materials;
* the identity of the constructed record.

It SHALL NOT:

* re-resolve artifacts;
* re-resolve relationships;
* reconstruct missing lineage;
* modify upstream artifacts;
* alter completeness findings;
* alter integrity findings;
* reopen execution adjudication;
* reopen continuity adjudication;
* recertify realization;
* establish final cycle-record authority;
* perform external verification.

---

# Preconditions

Construction SHALL NOT begin unless all of the following are true:

1. the Committed Realization Record exists and is uniquely identified;
2. the Canonical Artifact Resolution Set has a successful terminal status;
3. the Resolved Constitutional Relationship Set has status `RESOLVED`;
4. the Constructed Supervisory Lineage has status `CONSTRUCTED`;
5. the Lineage Completeness Evaluation Result has status `COMPLETE`;
6. the Lineage Integrity Evaluation Result has status `INTEGRITY_VALID`;
7. the Supervisory Cycle Record Definition is constitutionally fixed;
8. all required contract and schema versions are bound;
9. no Stage Two failure artifact has already been committed for the same cycle;
10. no Committed Supervisory Cycle Record already exists for the same commitment identity except through an authorized idempotent commitment path.

Failure of any precondition SHALL prevent construction.

# Audit Index Rules

The Supervisory Cycle Audit Index SHALL:

* preserve exact source identities;
* use deterministic key ordering;
* distinguish direct members from external references;
* distinguish artifacts from relationships;

* preserve unavailable or unresolved reference states where constitutionally permitted;
* contain no mutable “latest” or “current” pointer unless explicitly defined by a committed registry.

The index SHALL NOT create a new constitutional claim.

It points to claims already represented by committed or validated artifacts.

# Replay Boundary

The Supervisory Cycle Replay Manifest SHALL distinguish:

## Constitutional Replay

Re-evaluation of the constitutional transformations using the exact committed inputs, definitions, versions, and deterministic rules.

## Runtime Re-execution

A new attempt to execute a command in a runtime environment.

## External Verification

Independent evaluation by NullBlade or another constitutionally external authority.

These SHALL remain distinct:

```text
Constitutional Replay
≠
Runtime Re-execution
≠
External Verification
```

Section 22 constructs the replay manifest only.

Replay authority is governed by Section 26.

# Failure Consequences

If construction fails:

* no valid Constructed Supervisory Cycle Record SHALL exist;
* Supervisory Cycle Commitment SHALL NOT begin;
* no Committed Supervisory Cycle Record SHALL exist;
* downstream handoff SHALL NOT occur;
* Stage Two SHALL progress toward Committed Supervisory Provenance Failure.

Construction failure SHALL NOT authorize:

* partial record construction;
* omission of required components;
* artifact reconstruction;
* relationship inference;
* lineage repair;
* completeness override;
* integrity override;
* alternate schema selection;
* silent version migration;
* commitment of an incomplete record.

---

# Determinism

For identical:

* Committed Realization Record;
* Canonical Artifact Resolution Set;
* Resolved Constitutional Relationship Set;
* Constructed Supervisory Lineage;
* Lineage Completeness Evaluation Result;
* Lineage Integrity Evaluation Result;
* Supervisory Cycle Record Definition;
* contract versions;
* schema versions;
* canonical ordering rules;
* canonical serialization rules;
* hashing rules;

Supervisory Cycle Record Construction SHALL produce an identical Constructed Supervisory Cycle Record.

Formally:

[
ConstructCycleRecord(R,A,E,L,C,I,D)=S_c
]

For identical inputs:

[
S_c=S_c
]

No ambient state, mutable pointer, implementation-specific ordering, provider default, or wall-clock variation may change the canonical record content.

The construction timestamp MAY differ only where the Supervisory Cycle Record Definition excludes it from constitutional identity or defines its deterministic source.

---

# Immutability

Once constructed, the Constructed Supervisory Cycle Record SHALL be immutable.

A correction SHALL require:

* a new constructed-record identifier;
* a new constructed-record hash;
* explicit supersession lineage;
* preserved reference to the prior constructed record;
* a new commitment evaluation.

A corrected record SHALL NOT overwrite the earlier constructed record.

Neither constructed record is canonical until commitment succeeds.

---

# No Premature Consumption

The Constructed Supervisory Cycle Record SHALL NOT be consumed as authoritative cycle history.

It SHALL NOT be consumed by:

* Contract 012;
* downstream governance;
* future observation;
* external verification as though committed;
* archival systems requiring canonical cycle history;
* replay systems requiring committed authority.

Authorized internal consumers are limited to:

* Section 23 — Supervisory Cycle Commitment;
* Contract 011 failure handling;
* internal diagnostic and audit preparation where explicitly permitted.
