# SACS Contract 012 — External Verification Profile and Evidence Interface

## Section 19 — Verification Result Certification and Commitment

### Purpose

Section 19 establishes the constitutional process by which a Constructed Verification Result becomes the sole authoritative verification representation within SACS constitutional history.

Where Section 18 determines whether a foreign verification artifact may enter SACS constitutional custody and constructs its canonical internal representation, Section 19 determines whether that representation may become an immutable constitutional artifact of SACS.

Verification Result Certification establishes constitutional commitment.

It does not perform verification.

It does not reinterpret the external verifier's judgment.

It does not modify historical supervisory records.

Its sole responsibility is to certify and atomically commit SACS' constitutional representation of the externally issued verification judgment.

---

# Constitutional Question

> **Under what conditions does a Constructed Verification Result become the immutable Committed Verification Result within SACS constitutional history?**

Certification SHALL determine:

* constitutional admissibility;
* verifier authority;
* submission correspondence;
* claim correspondence;
* scope preservation;
* integrity preservation;
* commitment eligibility;
* commitment uniqueness;
* commitment atomicity;
* constitutional readiness for historical commitment.

---

# Constitutional Identity

The defining constitutional identity of Verification Result Certification is:

> **Verification Result Certification is the deterministic constitutional process that certifies an admitted verification result and commits SACS' immutable representation of that result without altering the externally issued verification judgment.**

Certification is not:

* verification;
* claim evaluation;
* evidence interpretation;
* verifier approval;
* governance approval.

Formally:

```text
Constructed Verification Result
≠
Committed Verification Result

Foreign Verification Judgment
≠
Committed Verification Result
```

The former is a foreign constitutional artifact.

The latter is SACS' authoritative constitutional representation of that artifact.

---

# Constitutional Authority

Contract 012 exclusively owns:

* verification result certification;
* certification eligibility;
* correspondence validation;
* commitment authorization;
* commitment atomicity;
* commitment idempotency;
* commitment lineage;
* historical commitment.

Contract 012 SHALL NOT:

* modify verifier conclusions;
* strengthen or weaken findings;
* reinterpret limitations;
* remove uncertainties;
* rewrite verifier scope;
* alter the externally issued judgment.

---

# Certification Inputs

Certification SHALL consume only:

* Constructed Verification Result;
* Verification Result Admission Record;
* Committed Verification Submission;
* Verification Submission Commitment Record;
* Verification Profile;
* Verification Requirement Set;
* constitutional lineage;
* registry snapshot references.

Only admitted verification results MAY participate in certification.

---

# Certification Eligibility

Prior to commitment, the Constructed Verification Result SHALL satisfy every constitutional precondition.

Eligibility SHALL verify:

* successful admission;
* authenticated verifier;
* valid constitutional custody;
* successful submission correlation;
* preserved integrity;
* preserved scope;
* preserved limitations;
* preserved uncertainties;
* complete constitutional lineage;
* commitment uniqueness.

Failure of any mandatory criterion SHALL terminate certification.

---

# Correspondence Validation

Certification SHALL establish one-to-one correspondence between:

* Committed Verification Submission;
* submitted Verification Claims;
* submitted Verification Evidence Package;
* submitted Verification Profile;
* Verification Requirement Set;
* Constructed Verification Result.

No committed verification result SHALL correspond to multiple committed submissions.

No committed submission SHALL silently acquire additional claims during certification.

---

# Scope Preservation

Certification SHALL verify that the committed result does not exceed the submitted constitutional scope.

Scope preservation SHALL validate:

* subject scope;
* claim scope;
* temporal scope;
* implementation scope;
* environment scope;
* version scope;
* evidence scope.

If the external verifier narrowed scope, the narrowed scope SHALL be preserved.

Certification SHALL NOT broaden scope.

---

# Integrity Preservation

Certification SHALL preserve:

* verifier identity;
* claim results;
* methods performed;
* reported limitations;
* reported exclusions;
* reported uncertainties;
* integrity proofs;
* provenance;
* submission identity;
* constitutional lineage.

Certification SHALL introduce no semantic modification.

---

# Commitment Uniqueness

Exactly one Committed Verification Result SHALL exist for one admitted verification operation.

Certification SHALL verify:

* unique verification-result identity;
* unique submission identity;
* absence of conflicting commitments;
* absence of duplicate commitments.

Duplicate constitutional commitments SHALL constitute failure.

---

# Commitment Atomicity

Certification SHALL execute as one indivisible constitutional operation.

Exactly one of the following SHALL occur:

* one immutable Committed Verification Result is produced; or
* one explicit Verification Result Certification Failure is produced.

Partial commitment SHALL be constitutionally prohibited.

---

# Commitment Idempotency

Repeated certification attempts using identical constitutional inputs SHALL produce identical constitutional results.

If an identical Committed Verification Result already exists, subsequent certification attempts SHALL resolve deterministically without creating duplicate constitutional artifacts.

Certification SHALL therefore remain replay-safe.

---

# Verification Result Certification Record

Successful certification SHALL produce exactly one immutable constitutional artifact containing:

```text
verification_result_certification_id
constructed_verification_result_id
verification_submission_id
verifier_identity
certification_status
correspondence_validation
scope_validation
integrity_validation
lineage_validation
commitment_timestamp
certification_authority
registry_snapshot_references
replay_identity
certification_hash
```

The Certification Record SHALL constitute the constitutional evidence that commitment has occurred.

---

# Canonical Committed Verification Result

Successful certification SHALL produce one immutable constitutional artifact containing:

* committed verification result identity;
* verification submission identity;
* verifier identity;
* claim-level verification results;
* methods performed;
* evidence references;
* reported limitations;
* reported exclusions;
* reported uncertainties;
* validity scope;
* validity interval;
* integrity bindings;
* provenance bindings;
* constitutional lineage;
* certification identity;
* replay metadata;
* cryptographic identity.

The **Committed Verification Result** SHALL become the sole authoritative SACS representation of the external verification judgment.

---

# Constitutional Sovereignty

Verification Result Certification SHALL preserve the constitutional sovereignty of both participating constitutional systems.

The following doctrine SHALL hold:

> **External verification authorities issue verification judgments. Only SACS may commit representations of those judgments into SACS constitutional history.**

Accordingly:

* the external verifier SHALL remain sovereign over its verification judgment;
* SACS SHALL remain sovereign over its constitutional history;
* neither constitutional system SHALL modify or commit artifacts belonging to the other;
* commitment SHALL preserve constitutional independence while establishing verifiable correspondence.

---

# Commitment Failure

Certification SHALL terminate immediately upon detection of:

* failed admission;
* verifier-authentication failure;
* submission-correlation failure;
* integrity failure;
* scope violation;
* correspondence failure;
* duplicate commitment;
* lineage inconsistency;
* atomicity failure;
* idempotency violation;
* constitutional incompatibility.

Certification failure SHALL produce one explicit **Verification Result Certification Failure** artifact.

No partial Committed Verification Result SHALL exist following failure.

---

# Fundamental Certification Invariants

For every admitted verification result:

* exactly one Committed Verification Result SHALL exist;
* exactly one Verification Result Certification Record SHALL exist;
* certification SHALL be atomic, singular, immutable, and idempotent;
* certification SHALL preserve verifier findings without reinterpretation;
* certification SHALL preserve identity, integrity, provenance, lineage, and scope;
* only SACS may commit constitutional representations into SACS historical records;
* identical constitutional inputs SHALL always produce an identical Committed Verification Result.

---

## Constitutional Transition

Section 19 completes the inter-constitutional trust exchange established by Contract 012.

The external verifier has exercised its sovereign authority to issue a verification judgment.

SACS has exercised its sovereign authority to admit, certify, and commit its own immutable constitutional representation of that judgment.

Neither constitutional authority has exercised authority over the other's constitutional history.

The following section, **Section 20 — Verification Record**, defines the permanent historical artifact that unifies the committed submission, the committed verification result, and their complete constitutional lineage into the authoritative historical record of external verification.
