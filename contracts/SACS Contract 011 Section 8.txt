SACS Contract 011 — Realization Certification, Supervisory Provenance, and Audit
Section 8 — Evidence Admissibility Evaluation
Purpose

Section 8 establishes the constitutional authority responsible for determining whether the validated committed evidence is admissible for Realization Certification.

Evidence admissibility is the determination that the committed evidence is constitutionally sufficient, complete, and eligible to support the construction of authoritative supervisory history.

This section answers exactly one constitutional question:

Is the validated committed evidence admissible for historical certification?

It does not determine the historical outcome, construct timelines, certify realization, or assemble provenance.

Constitutional Responsibility

Evidence Admissibility Evaluation SHALL:

evaluate the admissibility of validated committed evidence;
determine whether mandatory constitutional evidence is sufficient for certification;
preserve the constitutional authority of admitted evidence;
reject evidence that is constitutionally inadmissible;
produce one canonical admissibility determination.

Successful completion authorizes progression to Realization Timeline Construction.

Admissibility Requirements

Evidence SHALL be admissible only if all of the following conditions are satisfied:

every required committed artifact is present;
constitutional identity has been successfully validated;
constitutional binding has been successfully validated;
integrity references remain valid;
commitment identities remain unique;
constitutional versions are compatible;
registry references remain resolvable;
required supervisory context is complete.

Failure of any mandatory requirement SHALL render the evidence inadmissible.

Sufficiency Evaluation

Evidence admissibility is determined by constitutional sufficiency rather than quantity.

Accordingly, this section SHALL determine whether the committed evidence is sufficient to support historical certification.

Evidence SHALL NOT become admissible merely because:

additional optional evidence exists;
implementation metadata exists;
external runtime information exists;
heuristic reconstruction is possible.

Only constitutionally required committed evidence contributes to admissibility.

Admissibility Constraints

Evidence Admissibility Evaluation SHALL NOT:

infer missing evidence;
resolve historical ambiguity;
construct timelines;
classify realization outcomes;
determine supervisory correctness;
evaluate external truth;
construct provenance.

Evidence SHALL either satisfy constitutional admissibility or fail explicitly.

Evidence Preservation

Admission into historical certification SHALL preserve:

commitment identity;
constitutional authority;
supervisory context;
integrity references;
registry identity;
schema identity.

No admitted evidence SHALL be modified.

Admission authorizes use.

It does not authorize alteration.

Admissibility Failure

Representative constitutional failures include:

REALIZATION_EVIDENCE_INSUFFICIENT

REQUIRED_EVIDENCE_INCOMPLETE

CONSTITUTIONAL_CONTEXT_INCOMPLETE

INTEGRITY_VALIDATION_FAILED

REGISTRY_REFERENCE_UNRESOLVABLE

SCHEMA_REFERENCE_UNRESOLVABLE

COMMITMENT_CONTEXT_INCONSISTENT

EVIDENCE_NOT_ADMISSIBLE

When admissibility fails:

Realization Timeline Construction SHALL NOT begin;
historical certification SHALL terminate;
provenance construction SHALL NOT begin.

Failure SHALL be represented explicitly.

Constitutional Output

Successful completion SHALL produce exactly one:

Evidence Admissibility Determination

The Evidence Admissibility Determination SHALL contain:

realization-certification context;
admissibility status;
evidence sufficiency determination;
constitutional basis for admission;
validation references;
determination timestamp.

This artifact is an internal constitutional working artifact.

It SHALL NOT constitute a committed historical artifact.

Architectural Constraints

Evidence Admissibility Evaluation is an admissibility authority.

It SHALL NOT:

determine realization outcome;
certify realization history;
establish historical timelines;
construct Realization Records;
construct Supervisory Cycle Records;
perform replay;
perform audit.

Its sole constitutional responsibility is determining whether the validated evidence may enter historical certification.

Fundamental Admissibility Principle

The defining principle of this section is:

Only validated committed evidence that is constitutionally sufficient and admissible may enter Realization Certification. Contract 011 SHALL reject insufficient or inadmissible evidence explicitly rather than compensate for, reconstruct, or reinterpret deficiencies.
