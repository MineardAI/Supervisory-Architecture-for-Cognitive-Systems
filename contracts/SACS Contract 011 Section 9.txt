SACS Contract 011 — Realization Certification, Supervisory Provenance, and Audit
Section 9 — Realization Timeline Construction
Purpose

Section 9 establishes the constitutional authority responsible for constructing the canonical temporal representation of an admissible supervisory realization.

The Realization Timeline organizes committed supervisory evidence into one deterministic constitutional sequence from which historical outcome may later be determined.

This section answers exactly one constitutional question:

What constitutional sequence of committed events is established by the admissible evidence?

It does not classify realization outcome, certify realization, construct historical records, or assemble supervisory provenance.

Constitutional Responsibility

Realization Timeline Construction SHALL:

organize admissible committed evidence into one canonical temporal sequence;
preserve the temporal relationships established by preceding contracts;
construct one deterministic realization timeline;
explicitly preserve temporal uncertainty where represented by committed evidence;
produce one canonical realization timeline for subsequent historical evaluation.

Successful completion authorizes progression to Realization Outcome Classification.

Timeline Inputs

Realization Timeline Construction SHALL consume only:

the Evidence Admissibility Determination;
the admitted committed constitutional artifacts;
the realization-certification context established by preceding sections.

No additional runtime information, implementation state, or external evidence may be introduced.

Canonical Timeline Construction

The timeline SHALL preserve the constitutional ordering of committed supervisory events.

Representative events include:

Committed Execution Disposition
        │
        ▼
Committed Supervisory Continuity Record
        │
        ▼
Realization Certification Context

Where additional committed events are required by future constitutional revisions, they SHALL be inserted according to the constitutional ordering rules governing those artifacts.

No implementation-defined ordering SHALL supersede committed constitutional ordering.

Temporal Preservation

Realization Timeline Construction SHALL preserve:

event identity;
event ordering;
event causality as represented by committed evidence;
commitment timestamps;
supervisory cycle identity;
realization identity.

Temporal preservation SHALL NOT:

invent intermediate events;
reorder committed events;
merge distinct realizations;
suppress uncertainty represented by committed artifacts.
Temporal Uncertainty

Where committed evidence explicitly represents uncertainty regarding timing or ordering, that uncertainty SHALL remain represented within the timeline.

Contract 011 SHALL NOT resolve uncertainty through:

heuristic estimation;
implementation assumptions;
inferred sequencing;
statistical reconstruction.

Historical uncertainty SHALL remain constitutional history.

Timeline Constraints

Realization Timeline Construction SHALL NOT:

determine realization success or failure;
determine supervisory correctness;
certify historical outcome;
modify committed timestamps;
reconstruct missing temporal information;
infer absent historical events.

Its responsibility is limited to organizing admissible committed evidence into one canonical historical sequence.

Timeline Failure

Representative failures include:

REALIZATION_TIMELINE_INDETERMINATE

TEMPORAL_ORDER_CONFLICT

EVENT_SEQUENCE_AMBIGUOUS

COMMITTED_TIMESTAMP_CONFLICT

UNRESOLVABLE_TEMPORAL_DEPENDENCY

REALIZATION_CONTEXT_FRAGMENTED

Upon failure:

Realization Outcome Classification SHALL NOT begin;
Realization Certification SHALL terminate;
provenance construction SHALL NOT begin.

Failure SHALL be represented explicitly.

Constitutional Output

Successful completion SHALL produce exactly one:

Constructed Realization Timeline

The Constructed Realization Timeline SHALL contain:

realization-certification context;
canonical event sequence;
commitment references;
temporal relationships;
preserved uncertainty representations;
construction timestamp.

The Constructed Realization Timeline is an internal constitutional working artifact.

It SHALL NOT constitute committed constitutional history.

Architectural Constraints

Realization Timeline Construction is a temporal organization authority.

It SHALL NOT:

classify realization outcomes;
determine certification;
construct the Realization Record;
construct supervisory lineage;
perform replay;
perform audit.

It organizes history.

It does not certify history.

Outcome classification interprets that organization within the authority established by committed supervisory evidence.

Fundamental Timeline Principle

The defining principle of this section is:

Realization Timeline Construction SHALL deterministically organize admissible committed constitutional evidence into one canonical temporal sequence while preserving every committed temporal relationship, uncertainty state, and constitutional ordering without reconstruction, reinterpretation, or modification.
