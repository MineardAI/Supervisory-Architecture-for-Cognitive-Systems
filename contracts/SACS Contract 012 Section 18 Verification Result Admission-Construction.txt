# SACS Contract 012 — External Verification Profile and Evidence Interface

## Section 18 — Verification Result Admission and Construction

### Purpose

Section 18 establishes the constitutional admission process governing externally issued verification results.

Its purpose is to determine whether a foreign constitutional artifact issued by an authorized external verification authority may enter SACS constitutional custody for canonical processing.

Admission establishes constitutional admissibility.

It does not establish correctness.

It does not establish agreement.

It does not establish historical authority.

Only admitted verification results may be transformed into canonical SACS verification artifacts.

---

# Constitutional Question

> **May a foreign verification artifact issued by an external constitutional authority enter SACS constitutional custody and be transformed into a canonical constitutional representation?**

The answer SHALL be represented by exactly one canonical admission decision.

Only admitted verification results may proceed to construction.

---

# Constitutional Identity

The defining constitutional identity of Verification Result Admission is:

> **Verification Result Admission is the deterministic determination that an externally issued verification artifact possesses the identity, integrity, provenance, authenticity, scope, and constitutional admissibility required to enter SACS constitutional custody for canonical construction.**

Admission is not:

* verification;
* certification;
* commitment;
* historical modification;
* verifier approval;
* governance approval.

Formally:

```text
Received Result
≠
Admitted Result

Admitted Result
≠
Certified Result

Foreign Verification Judgment
≠
Constructed Verification Result
```

---

# Constitutional Authority

Contract 012 exclusively owns:

* verification-result admission;
* authenticity validation;
* submission correlation;
* admissibility determination;
* custody acceptance;
* canonical construction.

Contract 012 SHALL NOT:

* alter the externally issued judgment;
* reinterpret verifier conclusions;
* broaden verification scope;
* weaken reported limitations;
* remove uncertainties;
* create new verification findings.

---

# Admission Inputs

Admission SHALL consume:

```text
Foreign Verification Result
Committed Verification Submission
Verification Submission Commitment Record
Verification Profile
Verification Requirement Set
Submission Manifest
Verifier Identity
Verifier Credentials
Applicable Registry Snapshots
```

Where required, admission MAY also consume:

```text
Cryptographic Trust Material
Credential Revocation Information
Replay Protection State
Historical Submission Registry
```

Every consumed artifact SHALL possess stable identity.

---

# Admission Sequence

Admission SHALL proceed through the following constitutional sequence:

```text
Foreign Verification Result
        ↓
Identity Resolution
        ↓
Issuer Authentication
        ↓
Submission Correlation
        ↓
Integrity Validation
        ↓
Schema Compatibility
        ↓
Replay Protection
        ↓
Admissibility Evaluation
        ↓
Custody Acceptance
        ↓
Constructed Verification Result
```

No stage may be omitted.

---

# Identity Resolution

Contract 012 SHALL resolve:

* external verification result identity;
* submission identity;
* verifier identity;
* claim identities;
* evidence references;
* method references;
* profile identity;
* requirement-set identity.

Ambiguous identities SHALL prevent admission.

---

# Issuer Authentication

The issuing authority SHALL be authenticated.

Authentication SHALL validate:

* verifier identity;
* signing authority;
* credential validity;
* trust-domain membership;
* authorization status;
* applicable credential scope.

An unauthenticated issuer SHALL prevent admission.

---

# Submission Correlation

Every received result SHALL correlate to exactly one previously committed Verification Submission.

Correlation SHALL validate:

```text
verification_submission_id
verification_profile_id
verification_requirement_set_id
claim_set_hash
submission_hash
authorized_verifier_identity
```

A result that cannot be correlated SHALL NOT enter constitutional custody.

---

# Integrity Validation

Integrity validation SHALL verify:

* result hash;
* digital signatures;
* cryptographic attestations;
* required integrity mechanisms;
* transmission integrity;
* artifact completeness.

Integrity establishes authenticity.

Integrity does not establish correctness.

---

# Schema Compatibility

The received result SHALL conform to the Result Schema authorized by the committed Verification Profile.

Compatibility SHALL validate:

* schema identity;
* schema version;
* mandatory fields;
* result vocabulary;
* claim structure;
* method reporting;
* limitation reporting;
* uncertainty reporting.

Schema incompatibility SHALL prevent canonical construction unless an explicitly authorized compatibility translation exists.

---

# Replay Protection

Admission SHALL detect replayed foreign artifacts.

Replay protection SHALL validate:

* duplicate result identity;
* duplicate submission identity;
* duplicate verifier transaction;
* duplicate custody transfer;
* expired replay window where applicable.

Duplicate foreign results SHALL NOT silently overwrite previously admitted artifacts.

---

# Admissibility Evaluation

Admission SHALL evaluate whether the foreign result satisfies constitutional admissibility requirements.

Evaluation SHALL include:

* authenticated origin;
* authorized verifier;
* valid submission reference;
* integrity preservation;
* scope preservation;
* profile compatibility;
* requirement compatibility;
* schema compatibility;
* constitutional lineage.

Admission SHALL NOT evaluate whether the verification conclusions are favorable.

---

# Admission Classes

Every admission evaluation SHALL produce exactly one admission class.

The canonical classes SHALL include:

```text
ADMITTED
ADMITTED_WITH_LIMITATIONS
REJECTED
UNSUPPORTED
INCOMPATIBLE
UNRESOLVED
PROHIBITED
```

These classes govern constitutional processing only.

They SHALL remain distinct from verifier result classes.

---

# Custody Acceptance

Successful admission SHALL establish SACS constitutional custody over the foreign verification artifact.

Custody acceptance SHALL identify:

```text
custody_acceptance_id
foreign_result_id
verification_submission_id
accepting_constitution
accepting_authority
custody_start_time
accepted_scope
accepted_limitations
accepted_hash
```

Custody acceptance SHALL NOT transfer ownership of the foreign verification judgment.

---

# Constructed Verification Result

Successful admission SHALL produce exactly one:

```text
Constructed Verification Result
```

The Constructed Verification Result SHALL contain:

```text
constructed_verification_result_id
foreign_result_id
verification_submission_id
verifier_identity
claim_results
methods_performed
limitations
exclusions
uncertainties
validity_scope
validity_interval
construction_timestamp
construction_authority
construction_hash
```

The Constructed Verification Result is an internal constitutional representation.

It is not yet authoritative within SACS.

---

# Construction Constraints

Construction SHALL:

* preserve every verifier finding;
* preserve every limitation;
* preserve every exclusion;
* preserve every uncertainty;
* preserve every claim result;
* preserve verifier identity;
* preserve result scope.

Construction SHALL NOT:

* strengthen conclusions;
* weaken conclusions;
* remove unfavorable findings;
* infer omitted findings;
* normalize result meaning beyond schema-authorized translation.

---

# Admission Failure

Admission SHALL fail upon detection of:

* unresolved identity;
* authentication failure;
* integrity failure;
* submission mismatch;
* unauthorized verifier;
* prohibited schema;
* replay violation;
* constitutional incompatibility;
* custody conflict.

Failure SHALL produce exactly one **Verification Result Admission Failure** artifact.

No Constructed Verification Result SHALL be produced following admission failure.

---

# Fundamental Admission Invariant

For every foreign verification artifact presented to Contract 012:

* exactly one admission outcome SHALL exist;
* only admitted artifacts may be constructed;
* construction SHALL preserve the externally issued judgment without reinterpretation;
* admission SHALL establish constitutional custody without transferring ownership or external authority;
* identical admitted inputs SHALL produce identical Constructed Verification Results.

The Constructed Verification Result remains a pre-commitment artifact.

Only Section 19 may certify and commit it as the immutable **Committed Verification Result**, preserving SACS' exclusive authority over its own constitutional history.
