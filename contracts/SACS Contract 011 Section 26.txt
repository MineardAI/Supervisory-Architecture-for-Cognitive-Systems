SACS Contract 011 — Realization Certification, Supervisory Provenance, and Audit
Section 26 — Deterministic Replay Authority
Purpose

Section 26 establishes the constitutional authority governing deterministic replay of completed supervisory cycles.

Deterministic replay verifies that a previously completed supervisory cycle can be reproduced using only committed constitutional artifacts, without reconstructing, modifying, or reinterpreting supervisory authority.

Replay exists solely to demonstrate constitutional reproducibility, historical integrity, and implementation conformance.

Replay SHALL never create new supervisory decisions, modify historical records, or replace committed constitutional artifacts.

Constitutional Identity

Deterministic Replay is the constitutional process by which a completed supervisory cycle is reproduced exclusively from committed constitutional artifacts to verify historical equivalence and constitutional consistency.

Its defining constitutional identity is:

Replay SHALL reproduce constitutional history exactly as committed and SHALL never become a new source of constitutional authority.

Replay is observational.

Replay is never authoritative.

Constitutional Responsibility

Section 26 SHALL:

establish constitutional replay authority;
define replay inputs;
define replay outputs;
establish replay equivalence requirements;
define replay conformance obligations;
preserve constitutional determinism.

Section 26 SHALL NOT:

execute supervisory control;
reconstruct missing history;
reinterpret committed artifacts;
modify constitutional records;
authorize new supervisory actions;
certify audit findings.
Canonical Inputs

Replay SHALL consume exactly one of the following terminal Stage Two artifacts:

one Committed Supervisory Cycle Record; or
one Committed Supervisory Provenance Failure.

These artifacts SHALL be accompanied by every immutable committed constitutional artifact required to reproduce the supervisory cycle, including:

Committed Operational Profile;
Committed Reference State;
Committed Control Command;
Committed Translation Transaction;
Committed Execution Disposition;
Committed Supervisory Continuity Record;
Committed Realization Record.

Replay SHALL consume only committed constitutional artifacts.

No implementation state outside the committed constitutional record may participate.

Replay Authority

Replay SHALL possess no constitutional authority beyond verification.

Replay SHALL never:

authorize supervisory execution;
alter supervisory history;
replace committed artifacts;
generate alternate supervisory outcomes;
create new constitutional records.

Replay SHALL exist solely to verify that constitutional history remains reproducible.

Replay Equivalence

Successful replay SHALL demonstrate constitutional equivalence with the committed supervisory history.

Replay equivalence SHALL include verification of:

supervisory identity;
artifact identity;
commitment ordering;
provenance relationships;
constitutional lineage;
commitment hashes;
serialization identity;
contract versions;
schema versions.

Replay MAY differ in implementation details that possess no constitutional significance.

Replay Outputs

Replay SHALL produce one Replay Verification Result containing:

Replay Identity
Replay Identifier
Replay Timestamp
Replay Version
Replay Status

One terminal status:

REPLAY_EQUIVALENT
REPLAY_NOT_EQUIVALENT
Replay Evidence
Equivalence Verification Result
Identity Verification Result
Integrity Verification Result
Determinism Verification Result
Diagnostic Information

Where replay fails:

verified mismatch classifications;
affected constitutional artifacts;
replay diagnostics;
implementation observations.

Replay diagnostics SHALL possess no constitutional authority.

Equivalence Requirements

Replay SHALL verify:

identical constitutional identity;
identical supervisory lineage;
identical commitment relationships;
identical canonical hashes;
identical ordering semantics;
identical constitutional references.

Implementation-specific execution timing, storage layout, memory organization, or deployment topology SHALL NOT affect replay equivalence.

Determinism

Identical committed constitutional artifacts SHALL always produce identical replay results.

Replay SHALL remain deterministic regardless of:

hardware architecture;
operating system;
deployment platform;
storage implementation;
execution environment.
Failure Handling

Replay failure SHALL produce one Replay Verification Result with status:

REPLAY_NOT_EQUIVALENT

Replay failure SHALL NOT:

invalidate historical authority;
modify committed constitutional artifacts;
alter supervisory history;
authorize reconstruction.

Replay failure identifies an implementation or conformance discrepancy, not a constitutional change.

Relationship to Audit

Replay verification provides evidence that MAY be consumed by audit.

Replay SHALL NOT perform audit.

Audit SHALL independently evaluate constitutional conformance.

Replay and audit remain constitutionally distinct functions.

Preservation Requirements

Replay SHALL preserve:

constitutional identity;
supervisory identity;
historical ordering;
artifact relationships;
provenance;
commitment authority.

Replay SHALL never introduce additional constitutional meaning.

Prohibitions

Section 26 SHALL NOT:

execute supervisory control;
authorize runtime behavior;
modify committed artifacts;
reinterpret supervisory decisions;
reconstruct missing artifacts;
replace constitutional history;
certify regulatory compliance.

Section 27
Audit Authority and Audit Representation

Sections 24 and 25 establish the mutually exclusive constitutional outcomes of Stage Two.

Section 26 verifies that either outcome can be deterministically reproduced without altering constitutional authority.

Section 27 then evaluates those committed outcomes for constitutional, regulatory, and governance purposes.

Fundamental Constitutional Principle

Deterministic Replay SHALL reproduce a completed supervisory cycle exclusively from committed constitutional artifacts, preserving constitutional identity, supervisory authority, provenance, commitment ordering, and historical equivalence without creating, modifying, replacing, or reinterpreting constitutional history.
