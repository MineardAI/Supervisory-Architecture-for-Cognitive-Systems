SACS Contract 012 — External Verification Profile and Evidence Interface
Section 12 — Verification Evidence Resolution
Purpose

Section 12 establishes the constitutional process by which Verification Evidence Definitions are resolved into the exact set of evidence instances required to satisfy a committed Verification Requirement Set.

Where Section 11 defines the reusable constitutional classes of verification evidence, this section determines which specific evidence instances SHALL be used for a particular verification submission.

Verification Evidence Resolution is deterministic.

Given identical constitutional inputs, every conforming implementation SHALL resolve exactly the same Verification Evidence Set.

Section 12 therefore serves the same constitutional role that Candidate Profile Discovery serves in Contract 006.

It does not construct an evidence package.

It does not evaluate verification claims.

It does not validate evidence sufficiency.

It determines which exact evidence SHALL participate in verification.

Constitutional Question

Which exact evidence satisfies the committed Verification Requirement Set?

Verification Evidence Resolution SHALL determine:

required evidence;
optional evidence;
prohibited evidence;
admissible evidence;
authoritative evidence;
compatible evidence versions;
temporally valid evidence;
evidence integrity eligibility;
provenance eligibility;
resolution precedence; and
deterministic evidence selection.

Only evidence successfully resolved under this section MAY proceed to package construction.

Constitutional Identity

A Resolved Verification Evidence Set is one immutable, cycle-specific constitutional artifact that identifies the complete collection of evidence instances authorized to satisfy the committed Verification Requirement Set for a single verification submission.

Resolution establishes participation.

Resolution does not establish correctness.

Resolution does not establish claim validity.

Resolution does not establish verification success.

Constitutional Authority

Contract 012 SHALL exclusively own:

evidence discovery;
evidence eligibility evaluation;
evidence admissibility evaluation;
evidence compatibility evaluation;
evidence version resolution;
evidence precedence;
deterministic selection;
conflict detection;
duplicate elimination; and
construction of the Resolved Verification Evidence Set.

Contract 012 SHALL NOT:

create evidence;
modify evidence;
repair evidence;
reinterpret evidence;
fabricate missing evidence;
infer unavailable evidence;
replace missing evidence with alternatives unless explicitly authorized by the committed Verification Profile; or
alter evidence authority.
Resolution Inputs

Evidence Resolution SHALL consume only committed constitutional artifacts, including:

Committed Verification Applicability Result;
Committed Verification Requirement Set;
Committed Verification Profile Binding;
Verification Method Definitions;
Verification Evidence Definitions;
committed constitutional artifacts;
registry snapshots;
implementation artifacts;
execution provenance;
replay artifacts;
proof artifacts;
cryptographic attestations; and
any additional evidence explicitly authorized by the Verification Profile.

No uncommitted artifact SHALL participate in evidence resolution.

Evidence Eligibility

An evidence instance SHALL be eligible only if it satisfies every applicable constitutional requirement.

Eligibility SHALL include verification of:

evidence identity;
evidence definition;
evidence class;
commitment status;
integrity state;
provenance completeness;
temporal validity;
version compatibility;
lifecycle state;
constitutional authority;
supported verification methods; and
supported claim types.

Evidence failing any eligibility criterion SHALL NOT participate in further resolution.

Required Evidence

The Verification Requirement Set SHALL identify evidence that is mandatory.

Every required evidence item SHALL resolve to exactly one admissible evidence instance unless multiple instances are explicitly authorized.

Failure to resolve required evidence SHALL terminate evidence resolution.

No implementation SHALL silently omit required evidence.

Optional Evidence

The Verification Requirement Set MAY authorize optional evidence.

Optional evidence MAY strengthen verification capability but SHALL NOT alter constitutional meaning.

Optional evidence SHALL never satisfy a missing required evidence obligation unless explicitly authorized by constitutional policy.

Prohibited Evidence

Evidence SHALL be rejected whenever:

prohibited by the Verification Profile;
prohibited by the Requirement Set;
constitutionally superseded;
cryptographically invalid;
outside authorized provenance;
outside temporal scope;
incompatible with the verification subject;
incompatible with the verification method;
revoked;
corrupted;
incomplete; or
otherwise constitutionally inadmissible.

Prohibited evidence SHALL never appear in a Resolved Verification Evidence Set.

Evidence Admissibility

Evidence admissibility SHALL evaluate whether evidence may constitutionally participate in verification.

Admissibility SHALL evaluate:

authority;
provenance;
integrity;
commitment;
lifecycle state;
constitutional scope;
replay eligibility;
temporal validity; and
applicable constitutional restrictions.

Admissibility establishes constitutional participation.

It does not establish evidentiary sufficiency.

Evidence Compatibility

Evidence SHALL be compatible with:

the Verification Profile;
the Verification Requirement Set;
the Verification Method;
the Verification Subject;
supported claim types;
required evidence definitions;
constitutional versions; and
applicable registry snapshots.

Evidence incompatibility SHALL terminate resolution.

No implementation SHALL attempt automatic compatibility repair.

Temporal Compatibility

Every resolved evidence instance SHALL satisfy constitutional temporal requirements.

Temporal compatibility SHALL verify:

applicable constitutional versions;
registry snapshot identity;
commitment ordering;
replay ordering;
execution ordering;
validity intervals;
expiration policies; and
supersession rules.

Evidence outside authorized temporal boundaries SHALL be rejected.

Provenance Compatibility

Every resolved evidence instance SHALL preserve complete constitutional provenance.

Resolution SHALL verify:

producing contract;
producing artifact;
producing authority;
producing implementation;
producing registry snapshot;
lineage chain;
cryptographic identity; and
commitment lineage.

Evidence with incomplete or unverifiable provenance SHALL be rejected.

Resolution Precedence

Where multiple admissible evidence instances satisfy the same requirement, deterministic precedence SHALL be applied.

Precedence SHALL be established exclusively by constitutional policy.

Acceptable precedence criteria MAY include:

highest constitutional authority;
latest committed version;
strongest integrity guarantees;
highest provenance confidence;
canonical registry ordering; or
explicit profile precedence rules.

Implementations SHALL NOT employ nondeterministic selection.

Duplicate Resolution

Equivalent evidence SHALL be identified deterministically.

Duplicate evidence SHALL either:

collapse into one canonical instance; or
remain explicitly represented where constitutional meaning differs.

Duplicate elimination SHALL preserve provenance.

Resolution Conflicts

Evidence conflicts SHALL be explicitly represented.

Conflict resolution SHALL never modify evidence.

Conflict resolution SHALL either:

deterministically select one constitutionally authorized instance; or
terminate resolution with an explicit constitutional failure.

Implicit conflict resolution is prohibited.

Canonical Resolved Verification Evidence Set

Successful evidence resolution SHALL produce one immutable constitutional artifact containing:

resolution identifier;
submission identifier;
Verification Profile reference;
Requirement Set reference;
resolved evidence inventory;
required evidence inventory;
optional evidence inventory;
excluded evidence inventory;
prohibited evidence inventory;
evidence definitions;
evidence instance references;
provenance references;
integrity references;
compatibility results;
temporal validation results;
admissibility results;
conflict decisions;
duplicate decisions;
registry snapshot references;
constitutional lineage;
creation metadata; and
cryptographic identity.
Resolution Failure

Evidence Resolution SHALL terminate immediately upon any constitutional failure including, but not limited to:

missing required evidence;
inadmissible evidence;
incompatible evidence;
unresolved conflicts;
duplicate ambiguity;
invalid provenance;
integrity failure;
registry inconsistency;
temporal incompatibility;
unsupported evidence class; or
nondeterministic resolution.

Resolution failure SHALL produce an explicit constitutional failure artifact.

No partial Resolved Verification Evidence Set SHALL be produced.

Constitutional Invariants

For every committed Verification Requirement Set:

exactly one deterministic evidence resolution SHALL exist;
every required evidence obligation SHALL either resolve or explicitly fail;
no prohibited evidence SHALL participate;
every resolved evidence instance SHALL preserve constitutional identity, authority, provenance, integrity, and replay determinism; and
identical constitutional inputs SHALL always produce an identical Resolved Verification Evidence Set.
